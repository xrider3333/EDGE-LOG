2. THE FAIR-COMPARISON RULE
---------------------------
ROC / YR scales with contracts. Round 56 showed the same #397 at x0.5 / x1 / x1.5 / x2 gives 48.7 / 97.4
/ 146.1 / 194.8 %/yr. A plain "more ROC / YR" is therefore not a beat. Every candidate is scaled by ONE
book-wide factor k, with legs rounded to 0.1 contract because micro contracts make that tradeable. k is
chosen on the PRE-LOCKBOX stretch only, so that the candidate's pre-lockbox drawdown equals #397's
$34,449. That drawdown is the WORSE of the at-close and valued-daily readings. k is then carried
unchanged into the lockbox. Leg weights stay 1/1/3/1 before k: round 56 Test A found that re-weighting
has no forward skill.


3. THE PRE-REGISTERED BAR (written into PREREG_R58.txt before any candidate number existed)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At that k, a candidate beats #397 only if ALL of:
  1. pre-lockbox ROC >= 1.05 x 97.4 = 102.3 %/yr
  2. lockbox ROC >= 306.3 %/yr (at close) AND lockbox net valued daily >= $290,729
  3. lockbox drawdown at close <= 1.05 x $25,357 = $26,625 AND valued daily <= 1.05 x $49,855 = $52,348
  4. more net than #397 (scaled the same way) in >= 10 of the 15 calendar years 2011-2025, and no more
     than 3 years with less net AND a deeper in-year drawdown
  5. ML legs use ONLY the model each run's own gate_validate chose, at its chosen cut-off. The hybrid
     size_norm is frozen on pre-lockbox survivors, and scores are walk-forward (min_history 30,
     refit_every 25). No model, cut-off or weight shopping on the lockbox.
  6. it survives an adversarial check: look-ahead in the gate path, roll-step phantom P&L,
     concentration, and "is it just leverage".

The candidate family was also fixed in advance:
  - the four #397 slots, each as in #397 or with its run's own validated ML (cut or hybrid);
  - the NOISE slot as #382 (the validated compression re-size of #304), with or without its own gate;
  - at most ONE new validated leg, at weight 0.5 or 1.
Every book built is reported below, pass or fail.

In the tables, "k" is the scale factor. The two DD columns are the LOCKBOX drawdown at close and valued
daily. "yrs" is the number of years with more net / the number of years with less net and a deeper
drawdown. "fails" lists the bars missed.

9. ROLL AUDIT CAVEAT - THE FIGURES ARE PRE-AUDIT, THE VERDICTS STAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Every round-58 number was computed on the stored, unadjusted (no-adjust) masters. ROLL_AUDIT.md, written
later the same day, found that the ENGU-Q NQ leg carries roll phantom P&L:
  - #397's ENGU-Q leg crosses 36 contract switches and books $38,985 of offset.
  - On back-adjusted prices the leg falls from $603,381 to $499,155 (-17%), because the trade sequence
    changes too.
  - TTM #369 falls from $135,884 to $121,491, NOISE moves about 1%, and ORB is identical.
  - With all fixes #397 itself is $1,623,261 whole-run (was $1,773,541). Its pre-lockbox drawdown is
    $40,971 at close (was $33,567) and $41,853 valued daily (was $34,449).

The verdicts stand because both sides of every comparison carry the same legs on the same masters:
  - ORB #297 and TTM #369 are identical in #397 and every candidate.
  - Every ENGU-Q variant is the same 1,949 trades; a cut keeps a subset. The one exception is the 40
    58a books that swap in run #335's champion, a different configuration. The audit moves that one
    too (whole run $541,330 -> $476,435), and those books failed 3 or 4 of the 4 bars.
  - Every NOISE variant is #304's own trades, re-sized.
  - DIP on ES #425 is the one add-on only on the candidate side. The audit makes its drawdown larger
    ($66,592-$68,714, was $54,016), which only deepens its fail.
The 58d adversarial check also removed the booked roll steps (37 crossings, $39,387 by its method) from
both ENGU-Q legs, and the bar result did not change.

The ABSOLUTE figures are pre-audit and will move when the books are re-scored on corrected legs. That
covers 97.4 / 306.3, the $34,449 matching target, and every candidate ROC above. The ENGU-Q gate was
also trained on unadjusted prices, so a corrected leg would need its own bake-off.
