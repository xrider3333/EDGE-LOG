3. PRE-REGISTERED TESTS (written into tools/book56_analysis.py before any number was computed)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST A - does RE-WEIGHTING the four legs beat leaving them at 1/1/3/1, FORWARD, at equal risk?
  (The book-level twin of ORB_META_WF.txt. BOOK.md s5 tried weights once, in-sample, with one
  held-out read, and did not adopt them; this is the walk-forward version it never had.)
  12 forward years, July-June, Jul 2013 .. Jun 2025; the lockbox reported on its own. At each start,
  from the trailing 3 years only:
   A1 SELECT: 216 weight vectors (ORB 0.5-1.5, ENGU-Q 0.5-1.5, squeeze 1-6, NOISE 0.5-2), pick the best
      trailing MAR, scale to 1/1/3/1's trailing daily risk, round to 0.1 contract.
   A2 INVERSE-RISK RULE, no search: weight = 1 / the leg's trailing daily risk, same scaling.
   A3 NULL: 500 random vectors per year scaled the same way -> where A1's pick lands among them.
  PASS: more forward net than 1/1/3/1 in >= 8 of 12 years AND more total AND forward max drawdown
  no worse than 1.05x (A1 must also average above the null's 50th percentile).

TEST C - a FIFTH leg at weight 0.5 or 1: ENGU-Q R2 on ES (run #370's champion), the NQ 15m squeeze
  break (run #280's champion), and both. House bar (BOOK.md s10): annualised MAR >= 1.05x #397, LOCKBOX
  drawdown within 5%, lockbox net >=. Plus calendar years 2011-2025, dominate / lose vs #397.

5. THE BIGGER FINDING - EL'S BOOK DRAWDOWN MISSES ENGU-Q'S OPEN TRADES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A book curve books each trade on the day it CLOSES. Every leg in #397 is intraday except ENGU-Q, whose
trades stay open for days to months: on NQ, median 0.23 days but 90th percentile 4.6 days, longest 143
days, and the 182 trades held over 5 days carry $901,692 (the whole leg nets $603,381). While those
trades are open their swings never reach the book's curve.

tools/book56_mtm_check.py values every open ENGU-Q position at the last close of each day it is open
(same day stamp as the book). Per trade, the day-by-day increments sum exactly to the closed P&L, so
nothing is added or lost - only WHEN it is counted moves.

                                   closed-trade curve (EL today)     marked to market daily
  #397 pre-lockbox worst drop       $33,567 (trough 2020-03-27)      $34,449 (trough 2020-03-27)
  #397 LOCKBOX worst drop           $25,357 (trough 2025-12-11)      $49,855 (trough 2026-06-26)
  #397 lockbox net                  $306,042                         $290,729
  ENGU-Q NQ leg alone, pre drop     $38,872                          $48,599
  ENGU-Q NQ leg alone, LB drop      $39,550                          $48,413
  ENGU-Q ES #370 alone, LB drop     $7,108                           $31,708

  Worked example: the ENGU-Q long entered 2026-06-11 was up $36,184 on 2026-06-16 and closed on
  2026-06-24 at +$7,095. The account gave back $29,089 on that one position; the closed-trade curve
  only ever saw +$7,095.

  So #397's lockbox MAR is about 5.8 on an account basis, not the 12.1 EL shows, and $15,313 of the
  lockbox's booked profit was earned before the lockbox began. The pre-lockbox figures barely move
  because the worst stretch there (the 2020 crash) belongs to the intraday legs. This applies to EVERY
  stored book and validate that carries an ENGU-Q leg, and to any sizing decision made off them.

  NOT CHANGED: every stored book drawdown is on the closed-trade rule; switching would move them all
  at once (same situation as the day-stamping item in BOOK.md s10b). Owner call: add a marked-to-market
  reading beside the closed-trade one on book runs (RUNBOARD BOOKS row) - the engine has the bars; the
  daily mark is ~40 lines in augur_engine/book.py.

5b. A SECOND MULTI-DAY ARTEFACT - CONTRACT ROLLS ON THE NO-ADJUST MASTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measured by the sibling session "Strategy beating frontier models on ROC/y" (2026-09-24), on this
round's own leg parameters. The masters are no-adjust stitched series: at each quarterly roll the price
steps up by the carry between the two contracts (~1% of price when rates are high). An intraday leg
never holds across a stitch; a multi-week long does, and books the step as profit it never earned.

  ENGU-Q NQ leg (#397's):  23 trades cross a stitch, $39,580 booked = 6.6% of the leg's net
                           pre-lockbox 21 trades $26,595 | lockbox 2 trades $12,985 (12-15% of the leg's lockbox)
                           ~$0 in 2010-17 (rates near zero); 2022 $3,810, 2023 $7,750, 2024 $15,620, 2025 $4,745, 2026 $8,240
  ENGU-Q ES #370 leg:      30 trades cross, $33,912 = 8.9% of net; lockbox 4 trades $11,575 (a third of its
                           lockbox counted by entry date)
  Method: seam days from the house roll-seam detector on daily RTH bars; stitch bar = largest 1-minute
  open-vs-previous-close jump within 4 days before to 1 day after; a trade crosses when fill < stitch <=
  exit; booked = side x jump x multiplier. Approximate - each stitch bar is a Sunday open, so its size
  includes that weekend's gap - but 2022+ steps are consistently positive and scale with price (NQ
  +178..+412 points), i.e. the carry, so the bias is real.

  Together for #397's lockbox: booked $306,042 -> about $290,729 marked to market -> about $277,700 once
  the two roll steps are removed; worst drop $49,855. That is a lockbox ROC of roughly 278 %/yr at a MAR
  near 5.6, against the 306 %/yr and 12.1 EL prints today. Pre-lockbox: about $26,600 of $1,467,499.


6. VERDICT
----------
- #397 stays the FRONTIER book with the best ROC / YR; no pre-registered change beats it at equal risk.
- Weights stay 1/1/3/1 (Test A: 6 of 12 years, 49th percentile - no forward skill).
- No fifth leg: ENGU-Q on ES was one 347-day trade; the NQ 15m squeeze adds risk faster than money.
- More ROC / YR is available only by adding contracts, and the real drawdown per contract is larger
  than EL shows once ENGU-Q's open trades are counted - size off the marked-to-market figure.
- ENGU-Q's profit also carries roll steps it never earned (6.6% of the NQ leg, rising since 2022); a
  multi-day leg should run on a roll-adjusted master or book the step out.
