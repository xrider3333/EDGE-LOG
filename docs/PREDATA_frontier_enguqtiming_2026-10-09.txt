PART A - SPLIT "WHEN" INTO ENTRY TIMING AND EXIT TIMING (one choice each):
- ENTRY-ONLY TWIN: 1.0 NQ entered at each of the leg's own entry bars, but held for a duration taken from ANOTHER of the leg's WF trades (a
  random permutation of the leg's own trade durations, in bars), cut short at the leg's next entry so positions never overlap; valued
  close-to-close like Q31's in-position twin; 200 permutations, seed 20261009, the mean daily $ across them is the row.
- The chain (standalone ROC@30k and $ a year, DD5 beside; and each as a seat on L like Q31):
  always-on twin (drift at the average exposure) -> entry-only twin = ENTRY TIMING;
  entry-only twin -> in-position twin (the real exits, at bar closes) = STOP-EXIT TIMING;
  in-position twin -> the leg = fills (Q31: +1.6).
- Costs: the entry-only twin pays the leg's 0.783-pt round trip per trade like the in-position twin.

PART B - THE RTH-ONLY, OVERNIGHT-HOLD EXECUTOR (the Webull paper book, owner GO 10-09):
- Every entry and exit the leg signals at a bar inside RTH (a regular session's 09:30-15:59 ET bars) fills at that bar's close, as in
  Part A. A signal outside RTH (overnight / pre-market / a CME-holiday session) fills at the OPEN of the next regular session's 09:30 bar.
  Positions may be held overnight. Stop exits that trigger overnight therefore fill at the next open (gap risk included).
- Instrument: NQ itself at 1.0 contract (the QQQ executor's scale and share costs are the paper lanes'; this isolates the timing).
- Rows: the RTH executor's leg (the same trades, delayed fills); its entry-only twin (same delays); the counts and $ of signals outside RTH
  (entries / exits); then the Part A chain re-done for the executor: which of ENTRY / EXIT timing survives the delay, in ROC points and $.
- Also: the RTH executor as a seat on L (L with the executor's ENGU-Q in place of the leg), DD5 beside.
