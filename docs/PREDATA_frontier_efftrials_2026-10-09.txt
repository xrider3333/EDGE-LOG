- DISTANCE. d_ij = sqrt((1 - rho_ij) / 2), rho = the Pearson correlation of the two daily series on their common days.
- METHOD. ONC as published (Lopez de Prado & Lewis 2019): base clustering = k-means on the rows of the distance matrix for every k = 2 ..
  N - 1, 10 initialisations each (fixed seed 20261009), quality = mean / std of the silhouette scores, the best k kept; then the published

- CLUSTER RULE. E = the number of clusters ONC returns = the effective number of independent trials.

  SR0 = sqrt(V) x [(1 - g) x invPhi(1 - 1/N) + g x invPhi(1 - 1/(N e))], g = 0.5772; V = the variance of the cluster Sharpe ratios, a cluster's
  series = the inverse-variance-weighted mean of its members (daily Sharpe, not annualised); DSR = Phi((SR - SR0) x sqrt(T - 1) /
  sqrt(1 - skew x SR + (kurt - 1) / 4 x SR^2)). Printed at N = E_low, N = E_high and N = 71 (raw), with the same V.
