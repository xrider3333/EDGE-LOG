WHY. Last month's losers out-earn last month's winners the next month - the oldest stock anomaly, and in large caps the strongest form is the
RESIDUAL one: the part of last month's return not explained by the market. Blitz et al. find residual reversal has about twice the risk-
adjusted return of the raw form and far smaller crashes, because it does not load on the market in sell-offs. House evidence first: DDW's
L1 weekly reversal EARNED in #463's drawdown weeks but was long beta in crashes (long last week's losers = the wrong weeks); the scoping
doc's B5 line says only a beta-neutral form can fix that. Here the residual cell is that form. HONEST PRIORS: (1) a month-to-month sort
turns over most of its names (SEASON r1: 85-87%), so costs decide - the 20 bps row is the stress bar, as for SEASON; (2) the raw cell will
likely be long beta in sell-offs (L1's flaw) - beta is printed first; (3) the house's power line for this engine: a cell needs about
$17,500 a year of true edge for 50% power (SEASON / HIGH52 power addenda).

THE ENGINE = RESMOM r1's (r17, imported unchanged) through the r25 / r26 pattern (the scores and the null's streams swapped, nothing else),
under MANAGER #127's hygiene rule S1 ('close'), run before main by the 10-08 rule if the queue is long, r17 pinned (3151ef0a) until main

Stage A rules (a)-(f) as RESMOM, except the stress check is at 20 bps (turnover).

THE TWO CELLS (a family of 2), each long the 50 LOWEST and short the 50 HIGHEST scores (the reversal direction), dollar-neutral, monthly:
- REV: the name's split-safe total return over the last 21 sessions (r-20 .. r), the month RESMOM skips.
- RREV: the same 21 sessions' RESIDUALS - each daily return minus beta x ES's return, beta from RESMOM's own regression window (the OLS
  beta r17's RES score already computes, r-251 .. r), summed and divided by their standard deviation over those 21 sessions (Blitz et al.'s
  scaling; CHOICE: the fitted alpha is not subtracted, as RESMOM v2).
Both cells rank one shared pool (a name needs both scores). Ties by symbol.
STAGE A: RESMOM r1's (a)-(f) on the 'close' reading, the stress check at 20 bps; FAMILY NULL = the MAX over both cells per draw (codes 29 REV
/ 30 RREV, 500 draws). POWER LINE FIRST: the null alone, committed as an addendum before stage_a (the r25 machinery).
