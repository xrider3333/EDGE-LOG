Q14 SECTOR BAB - betting against beta across the nine sector funds, beta-neutral (Frazzini and Pedersen 2014: leverage-constrained
  investors overpay for high beta, so low-beta assets earn more per unit of risk; a beta-neutral long-low / short-high position should

  sectors and short the 3 highest, equal notional within a side, each side scaled by 1 / its average beta so the position is
  beta-neutral (dollar-unbalanced), from the next open, held a month; 2 bp a side, borrow 0.25 %/yr on the short notional. Data:
