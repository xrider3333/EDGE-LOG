BOOK QUEUE Q20 (scoping item A1) - SEAT CAPACITY: how much volatility share can a monthly stock-basket seat take on top of the RESMOM
line before the book's ROC at $30k peaks, and how many such seats fit? Approved by MANAGER #90 (GO with one edit = ADDENDUM 1
below), committed 2026-10-05 before any number of this item. A planning map, not a test: synthetic seats only, no real candidate is read. Walk-forward only.

THE BOOK. L = #463 + 0.264 x RES (BOOK.md 10ab, built at parity in Q19: 120.82 / 3.916 / $36,526). Its drawdown days R = the house rule
(peak-to-trough >= 1/3 of the deepest, day after the peak through the trough: 45 episodes, 762 days).
THE GENERATOR. MDL r1's own (tools/rocfrontier/r12_mdl.py gen_leg, imported, not copied): a seat active on a day with probability
min(1, n/252), P&L = mu + sigma g_t (rho_r z_t + sqrt(1 - rho_r^2) e_t), z = L's regime-standardised P&L (regime_z on R vs the rest),
g = L's trailing-20-row volatility normalised (norm_g, 'clustered'), e Student-t(3); mu re-centred as MDL r1's addendum 1.
THE GRID (fixed now). Seat quality s (MDL's Sharpe-like target) in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75}; behaviour on R: rho_dd in {-0.3 (earns while L
falls, RESMOM-like), 0.0 (unrelated)}, rho_out = 0; n = 252 (a daily-marked monthly basket is always on); tails t3; vol clustered.
Size per seat: volatility share c in {0.00, 0.05, ..., 1.00} x L's daily SD. Number of seats N in {1, 2, 3, 4}, drawn INDEPENDENTLY of
each other (cross-seat correlation 0; a second row with cross-seat correlation 0.3 is reported). 2,000 draws per (s, rho_dd, N), numpy
default_rng([20261020, cell index]).
PRINTED. For each (s, rho_dd, N): the median and 25-75% band of L + sum(c x X_i) on ROC at $30k and Sortino over the c grid (all N seats
at the same c); c* = the c with the highest median ROC; the ROC at c*; and the share of draws whose ROC at c* beats L. The headline table:
"N seats at share c* lift the line to ROC r (band) - the n-th seat adds d". The 71-look band of Q17 (the pooled +64% over 93.81, i.e. the
line's own looks start a new count) is printed beside, as a reminder that a synthetic lift is not evidence.
WHAT IT DECIDES. Only the SIZE rule and the seat count the basket preregs will use (scoping doc 5): the c that later seats are sized at,
and whether the queue aims at one more seat or several. Nothing is adopted.
