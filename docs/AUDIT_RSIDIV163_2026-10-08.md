# AUDIT - RSIDIV #163: why the lockbox shows ~2,260 NQ points a trade (DISC for MANAGER #58, 2026-10-08)

**Verdict: no units, look-ahead or engine bug. The numbers are real engine output on genuine prices. #163 is LEVERAGED
BUY-AND-HOLD with no timing skill, plus a small, already-known roll defect. Void it as a strategy; the reason is design,
not a broken run.**

## 1. The numbers reproduce
- Re-run of the champion (`RSIDIV_1_0.py`, NQ 5m RTH no-adj master id 37, cost 0.533) on the lockbox window alone
  (2025-07-16 .. 2026-07-16), which is how the validate computes its lockbox: **12 trades, 27,112.4 points**, matching
  #163's stored lockbox P&L (27,112.354) exactly. The stored lockbox figures are POINTS ($542k at $20 a point).
- **The prices are genuine.** The master's NQ against QQQ (Alpaca, an independent source), over 270 shared days:
  daily-return correlation 0.993. 2026-03-31 .. 05-29: NQ +27.1%, QQQ +27.9%.

## 2. The cause is the champion's settings
- `sl_type = PERC`, `stop_loss = 12.5`: a trailing stop 12.5% below the price, about 2,500-3,800 NQ points. It
  almost never fires.
- `max_pyramid = 4`: up to 4 stacked long lots. #163 is the ONLY one of 435 saved runs with stacked lots.
- Exits come from the RSI-83 cross or a bear divergence, so holds run for months (median 80 days, max 237), cut only
  at the forced quarterly roll flatten.
- **Result:** over the optimize window (2010-06 .. 2025-07-15) it is in the market 96% of days with 3.65 long lots on
  average. It earns 57,637 points without roll gaps, which is **0.84x** a constant 3.65-lot buy-and-hold of NQ (68,617
  points). Its lockbox year is 1.28x, one year.
- The lockbox's ~2,260 points a trade is 4 lots riding the +20-27% NQ rally of March-June 2026 (+4,744 .. +6,144 points
  a lot) and the July-December 2025 drift.

## 3. A second, smaller defect (already known: ROLL_AUDIT.md, 2026-09-25)
- The file finds contract rolls with the house `detect_roll_seams`: the biggest overnight gap near each quarterly
  expiry, needing >= 2.5x the 60-session median gap.
  - In volatile quarters a news gap wins and the real roll is held. For example, it flags 2025-03-10 (a -343 point gap)
    but the real roll was 03-18 (+208); 2024-03 is flagged a week late; 2023-03 is not flagged at all.
  - It needs 60 prior sessions, so a lockbox-only or fold-only window cannot see its first roll. #163's lockbox lots
    rode the September 2025 roll (+237 points x 4).
- **Fake roll points in #163:** lockbox 949 (4% of its P&L); full window 3,791 (5%). It is real but small, and it is
  not what makes #163 look large.

  since 2017-18), and drawdowns read too small when a flagged news gap is dropped (DIP 1.1, ROLL_AUDIT 3.6).
