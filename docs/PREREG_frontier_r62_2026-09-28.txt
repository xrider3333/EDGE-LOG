V - BOOK VOLATILITY TARGETING (Moreira & Muir 2017, volatility-managed portfolios). Mechanism: volatility clusters but the book's return does not rise

    rule that has held up here (NOISE #422: more size when the squeeze is compressed).
    m(d) = clip(TARGET / vol20(d), 0.5, 2.0), rounded to 0.1 (micro contracts), applied to EVERY trade by its entry day d (all four legs).
    vol20(d) = std of #463's own valued-daily P&L over the 20 index days strictly before d. TARGET = median vol20 over the IS days. m = 1 if undefined.
    Sensitivity (not selection): lookback 10 and 60; caps 0.5-1.5.
    Could fool us: this book earns most in turbulent regimes (2020, 2022), so scaling down there may cost more than it saves; ORB's same-day
    vol sizing is already dead (different signal: that one used today's range).

O - ORB x NOISE OVERLAP CAP. Mechanism: ORB and NOISE are both NQ day-session opening-momentum trades (daily corr 0.42); when both are in the same
    direction at once the book holds the same factor twice. Rule: a trade of either leg that ENTERS while a trade of the other leg is OPEN IN THE SAME
    DIRECTION is taken at half size. Opposite-direction overlaps and every other leg untouched. Sensitivity (not selection): skip instead of half.
    Could fool us: agreement days may be the strongest trend days, where the doubled exposure is exactly what earns.
    Tie rule (added before any run): if an ORB and a NOISE trade enter on the same bar in the same direction, the NOISE trade is the one halved.
