THE TWO CELLS (fixed now; a family of 2).
- BETA: the score is RESMOM's own OLS beta of the name's daily total return on ES's over the 252-session window ending at the rank close
  (the same pairs, the same >= 230 rule). Long the 50 LOWEST-beta names, short the 50 HIGHEST. Beta-neutral at the fill, gross $400,000

- VOL: the score is the sample SD (ddof 1) of the name's finite daily total returns over the same 252-session window. Long the 50 LOWEST,
  short the 50 HIGHEST, dollar-neutral, $4,000 slots (RESMOM's sizing).
