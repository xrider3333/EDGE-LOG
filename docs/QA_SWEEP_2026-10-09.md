## Cautions for future consumers
- tools/fetch_edgar_calendar.py:105-111 maps a 09:30-16:00 acceptance to the SAME session ("intraday"; BDay ignores
  holidays). Today's users only report it; anyone who uses that session's open or full-day return as "after the
  release" will leak.
- tools/orb_volarm_10s.py:42-46 buckets END-stamped 10s rows as if start-stamped: bars sit 10 s early (a lag, not a
  lead; harmless, but they will not match the master).
- Price LEVELS on back-adjusted futures: round-number work (round_r1_stageA, r57 anatomy) and DIP's constant-notional
  contract count already use the no-adjust masters (db_noadj_*); keep it that way.
