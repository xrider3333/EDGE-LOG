   #463's other drawdowns are LEG-SPECIFIC (ORB / NOISE / TTM's own bad stretches), so no market-stress trigger (VIX inversion,

   ONMOM, XASSET, COT, AUCTION all dead or under the line). Effects that are real there (AUCTION pre-auction short, t 2.0) are

## Addendum A (2026-10-05 15:10 MST) - INSIDER event COUNTS from the Form 4 file, before any return exists

No price or return was read (the member bars are not pulled yet); these are counts only, for the power line and the cell design.
Event = one issuer x Form 4 filing date with an open-market PURCHASE (code P) by an officer or director, inside that company's
Nasdaq-100 membership spell (tools/data/ndx_members.csv, joined on ndx_tickers), filed 2016-07-01 .. 2025-06-29.
Cohen-Malloy-Pomorski classes from each insider's own P / S history (owner_cik): routine = traded in the same calendar month in
each of the three prior years; opportunistic = traded in each of the three prior years, not routine; unclassified = shorter history.

- **650 events in the walk-forward window (~72 a year)**, 111 companies; per year 37 (2016 H2) / 91 / 93 / 68 / 66 / 70 / 89 / 68 / 47 / 21 (2025 H1).
- Opportunistic 99, routine 39, unclassified 512; clustered (2+ insiders the same day) 49; median event value $306k; 468 events >= $100k.
- Concentration: top 10 companies hold 38 % of events (FAST 55, KDP 44, INTC 37, DLTR 28, VRSK 16, AKAM 16, AVGO 13, WBA 13, WBD 13, ATVI 13).

Consequences for the INSIDER r1 draft (decided now, before returns):
1. The PRIMARY cell has to be ALL officer / director purchase events (~72 a year). The published opportunistic class (~11 a
   year) and the clustered class (~5 a year) cannot reach 50 lockbox trades; they are REPORTED splits, never cells.
2. The lockbox year will hold roughly 45-70 events: the 50-trade lockbox bar is at risk on the count alone - the prereg states
   that up front and asks MANAGER whether a short count fails the leg or reads as underpowered.
3. Per-company concentration is reported (top-10 share of net, leave-one-company-out), since four companies carry a quarter
   of the events.
4. With ~72 independent bets a year, the power line must come from the family null on these exact event dates (500 draws of
   random dates in the same company spells), not from a literature effect size.

## Addendum B (2026-10-05 15:25 MST) - map checks on the rest of the queue, before drafting (counts only, no returns)

Standing order 10-04 item 4: an idea that cannot plausibly earn ROC >= 15 at a $30k drawdown of its own is not drafted.
Rough size rule used for stock-event legs here (stated, not fitted): N positions a year x $50k x net edge per position, against
a drawdown of ~1.2-1.5 x the yearly P&L standard deviation of ~sqrt(concurrent positions) x $900 a day (1.8 % idiosyncratic).

1. **EAR r1 (post-earnings drift) - NOT DRAFTED.** 3,556 member earnings releases in the walk-forward window (~395 a year,
   3,344 with bars; 2,445 after the close, 1,067 before the open). Trading the top and bottom terciles (~260 positions a year,
   ~45 concurrent at a 60-session hold) needs >= ~0.4 % net drift per position to reach 15; large-cap drift after 2010 is near
   zero in the literature (Martineau 2021) - prior 5-12, under the line.
2. **8-K EVENT r1 (5.02 / 1.01 / 2.05 drift) - NOT DRAFTED.** Few events per item in mega-caps; prior 3-8 (scope rank 5).
3. **Opportunistic insider SELLING (short side) - NOT DRAFTED as its own leg.** 15,608 officer / director sale events in the
   window (1,734 a year): routine 4,834, opportunistic 3,064 (~340 a year, 2,020 distinct company-months, 134 companies).
   Published opportunistic-sale effects are about half the buy effect and weaker in large caps; at ~0.3 % a month, 224
   positions a year read ~ROC 8 after 10 bp round-trip costs and borrow - under the line. Kept as a REPORTED split for INSIDER
   (does a sale cluster in the 30 days before a purchase change the purchase's result) only if MANAGER asks.

**Result: INSIDER r1 is the only stock-event mechanism in this lane with a plausible shot at the map line** (EAP, the other one,
is STRATEGY-BEATING's). TV's queue therefore becomes: (1) INSIDER r1 Stage A; (2) second reviewer on STRATEGY-BEATING's EAP /

Why it fails the map (ROC >= 15 at a $30k drawdown of its own):
1. The items with enough events carry NO SIGN without reading the filing text: 5.02 mixes routine director appointments with CEO
   exits, 8.01 / 7.01 are catch-alls, 1.01 is any contract. A leg needs a direction; the item code alone does not give one.
2. The items with a plausible sign are far too rare: 2.05 restructuring 4 a year, 2.06 impairment 1, 2.01 acquisitions 10 -
   under 50 lockbox trades by an order of magnitude.
3. The house's own bound: INSIDER r1, a cleaner signed event (~72 a year, the literature's strongest event effect), read ROC 1.9
   with a post-event LAG; the 8-K literature (e.g. Lerman & Livnat 2010) puts most of the reaction on the filing day itself,
   with small drift in large caps - prior 3-8 (scope rank 5), under the line.

and still faces point 3; not proposed. TV's queue moves to the catalog mechanisms only when a prereg names their data.
