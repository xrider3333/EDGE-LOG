MECHANISM. When equity markets are in stress, the Fed's reaction function makes the FRONT of the Treasury curve rally harder than the
long end (cuts are priced at the short end; the long end also carries inflation and term-premium risk): the curve bull-steepens - 2008,
August 2011 to a lesser degree, 2019, March 2020, the 2024-25 cutting turns. A DURATION-NEUTRAL steepener (long short-duration Treasuries,
short long-duration Treasuries in the ratio of their durations) holds no level bet, only the slope bet, so unlike Q13's long TLT it does
not depend on long yields falling - the failure that broke the stock-bond hedge in 2022 (long yields rose) hurts a level bet, not a
duration-neutral slope bet as directly. Literature: the slope's response to monetary easing (e.g. Gurkaynak, Sack and Swanson 2005); the
"flight to the front end" in risk-off.
