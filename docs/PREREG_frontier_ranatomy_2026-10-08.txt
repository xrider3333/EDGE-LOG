WHAT IS COMPUTED (held data only; each line a count or a dollar sum on R; no new P&L series):
1. R's days classified, one class per day, by market state known at that day's open (no look-ahead into the day's own move):
   (a) ES trend: the last ES daily close strictly before the day (the roll-corrected ES 5m RTH master through r15's load_es, the last
   bar of each US/Eastern date) above / at-or-below the mean of its last 200 closes; (b) volatility: the last VIX close strictly before the
   day, by the WF stretch's terciles of the daily VIX closes (CHOICE: descriptive breakpoints from the whole stretch - a map, not a rule);
   (c) the VIX term structure: VIX / VIX3M of that prior close >= 1 'inverted', else 'normal'; (d) the day itself is a scheduled FOMC
   decision day (tools/data/fomc_dates.txt, the Fed's own list), else a CPI day (tools/data/cpi_dates.csv), else an NFP day
   (tools/data/nfp_dates.csv), else 'none'. A day whose prior state is undefined is its own class.
   Each day = a row of #463's index inside the WF stretch (the book's UTC day stamps); R = the S1 line's house drawdown days (r12's
   Stretch, 45 episodes / 762 days, checked before any table).
2. Per class: R days, L's dollars, and each leg's dollars (ORB, ENGU-Q, TTM, NOISE, 0.264 x RES) - so the table shows which leg loses in
   which state.
3. The same table on the days that are NOT in R (the leg's normal earning states), for contrast.
4. A shortlist, written AFTER the table and labelled as judgement: which published mechanism earns in the classes that carry most of R's
   loss, with its data need and whether the house holds it - the candidates for the next prereg (each still needs its own prereg, power
   line and Stage A).
