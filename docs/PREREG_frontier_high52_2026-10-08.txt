BOOK QUEUE Q25 (scoping item B3) - HIGH52 r1: the 52-week-high effect in single US stocks (George-Hwang 2004), as a monthly basket SEAT on the
RESMOM line, judged INCREMENTAL over RESMOM. PRE-REGISTRATION, FRONTIER, 2026-10-08 (draft v1 to MANAGER 11:20 MST; v2 = MANAGER's review
#116 (B), GO WITH 2 EDITS, folded in below; queued AFTER SEASON r1), committed before any number of this item. Walk-forward only (owner
decision b, 10-05). SCOPE_DISC 10-06 lists it as FRONTIER's; STRATEGY-BEATING's scope row 17 (52WH) names it RESMOM's sibling.

WHY. George and Hwang (2004) found that a stock's price relative to its own 52-week high predicts its return better than its past return
does, and that its profits do not reverse later (anchoring: traders under-react to good news near a high). It is the momentum family's
best-documented refinement. HONEST PRIOR, stated first: it will likely earn on the same days as RESMOM and lose in the same momentum crashes
(the scoping doc: "likely same days as RESMOM"), so the test that matters is the SEAT read over L - does it add money on L's drawdown days
(R), not standalone. A second cell takes out what L already holds, so we see whether anything is left beyond RESMOM's own signal.

THE ENGINE = RESMOM r1's (tools/rocfrontier/r17_resmom.py, imported, never edited) through FRONTIER's r19 / r25 pattern (the scores and the
null's streams swapped, nothing else), judged under MANAGER #127's hygiene rule S1 = r17's post_mode 'close' ([HYG-S1]). Its universe
(the 500 largest by trailing dollar volume, prior raw close >= $10), pool (>= 230 own returns and >= 230 ES pairs in the 252-session
window), month-end rank / next-open fill / month-end exit schedule, 50 a side at $4,000, costs, borrow, dividends and Stage A rules (a)-(f)
are used as is. Runs only once that r17 is on main.

THE TWO CELLS (a family of 2), each long the 50 HIGHEST and short the 50 LOWEST scores, dollar-neutral, monthly:
- PTH: the name's split-safe PRICE index at the rank close / its highest close over the 252-session window (r-251 .. r). The price index is
  the product of (1 + the split-safe price return, no dividends) - a [D2] spin-off / stock-dividend ex-date session is left out, as RESMOM
  leaves it out of its scores. Range (0, 1]; 1 = at the high.
- PTHX (MANAGER edit 1: take out what L already holds - L carries RESMOM's RESIDUAL score, not raw 12-1): at each rank, the residual of a
  cross-sectional OLS of PTH on an intercept, RESMOM's RAW score (the 12-1 split-safe total return, r17's 'RAW') AND RESMOM's RES score
  (the residual-momentum score L holds, r17's 'RES'), over the universe names with a full window (r17's two counts >= 230) and all three
  values (the pool is decided after the scores, so this is the pool before its hygiene / fill / audit exclusions). CHOICE: no winsorising.
Ties by symbol, as RESMOM. Both cells rank the same pool (a name needs PTH, RAW, RES and the pool's counts).

STAGE A (the research row; house line #45): RESMOM r1's rules (a)-(f) verbatim, on the 'close' reading. FAMILY NULL: the MAX over both
cells per draw (r17's null_summary; each cell draws its own random-name books, codes 25 (PTH) and 26 (PTHX) on RESMOM's scheme
[20261005, code, vcode], 500 draws). A cell that fails is DEAD as a leg; its seat numbers are still printed. A passing cell's audit
candidates (its top 50 name-months) are then checked as RESMOM's (f) - until then it reads 'PASS (audit pending)'.
POWER LINE FIRST (MANAGER edit 2): before any cell is run, `r26_high52.py power` runs the family null ALONE on the 'close' pools (the same
draws Stage A makes) and prints, per cell: the bar (ROC @ $30k >= max(15, the MAX-over-cells null p95)) and the true edge it takes to
clear it at 50% and 80% power in OWN MONEY ($ a year at the cell's size) and as $ a year at a $30k worst drawdown (= the bar's ROC x
$1,000); and for the seat, the null's dollars on R (p50 / p95 / SD) and the lead on R a seat needs (p95 - p50 at 50%; + 0.842 SD at 80%),
at own size and at the 0.25 seat. Those lines are committed as docs/PREREG_frontier_high52_2026-10-08_power.txt (quoting
high52_power.json's sha256) BEFORE stage_a; stage_a refuses until that file is committed.
THE SEAT READ (REPORT; the owner's call; BOOK.md 10ab via the seat pipeline): L (S1, file resmom_cells_daily_wf_close.csv, sha256

high is held split-safe) - stated, not tested.
