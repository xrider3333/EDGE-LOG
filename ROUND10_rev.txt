ORB ROUND 10 - FAILED-BREAKOUT REVERSAL ("trap trade") - READ (2026-09-08)
========================================================================

Smoke test: PASS. reverse_after_stop=False (all new knobs at default) is BIT-IDENTICAL
to ORB_3_6.py on the #314 crown params (2299/2299 trades, pnl matches to 1e-9).
reverse_after_stop=True measurably changes results (344 reversal trades fired).

rev_direct: NOT IMPLEMENTED. The spec's described scenario (first trade never taken
because trade_mode/First-candle direction forbade it, then price closes through the
OTHER side) turns out to already be exactly what the base entry loop does on its own:
"First-candle dir" mode fixes long_ok/short_ok for the WHOLE session before the k-loop
runs, and the allowed-direction close-cross is checked on every bar regardless of what
an earlier forbidden-direction breakout did — so if the allowed side ever closes past
its level, ORB_3_6's own logic already takes that trade normally. There is no daylight
left for a "direct" add-on without inventing an ungrounded rule (e.g. re-triggering the
FORBIDDEN direction itself, which the spec never describes). Left un-coded per the
spec's own "skip this if it makes the code messy" clause; the knob is present in
DEFAULT_PARAMS as an inert placeholder. No "2 best with rev_direct=True" rows, per spec.

5-BULLET READ
-------------
1. ALL 12 configs FAIL the gates, every one on G1 (5-year MAR never beats the crown's
   2.79 — best is 2.65, "rev/be=True/sf=1.5/tR=0"), most also on G1(full MAR>0.85, best
   0.84) and/or G3 (OOS PF: the crown's own OOS PF 1.531 is never matched, best 1.527).
   No adoption case here.
2. The reversal ADDS trades and net $ (up to +$33,086 full-window net on the best
   config) but adds them DISPROPORTIONATELY MORE drawdown (crown $28,857 -> $31,666 or
   $32,579, a ~10-13% DD jump) and it front-loads into the OOS year LESS than the base
   strategy does, which is exactly what drags MAR and the OOS gate down even as full
   net rises. This is a classic "more trades, worse risk-adjusted" pattern.
3. The trap trade DOES have standalone edge, and it is real, not noise: on its own
   (n=344-408 depending on reverse_after_be), PF ranges 1.006-1.252 and every single
   one of the 12 sweep configs' reversal-only PF is >= 1.0 (positive expectancy before
   even blending with the first trade). The best slice (be=True, rev_stop_frac=1.5,
   rev_target_R=0->reuse target_R) nets $33,086 on 408 trades at PF 1.252, win% 35.8 -
   a real but modest, low-win-rate/long-tail edge (consistent with "ride the failed
   breakout's unwind" being a trend continuation, not a mean reversion, bet).
4. rev_stop_frac matters MUCH more than rev_target_R: sf=1.5 beats sf=0 (reuse
   stop_frac=2.5) and sf=2.0 on every single row of every window - a TIGHTER stop on
   the reversal (1.5x range vs the parent's 2.5x) is uniformly better. rev_target_R
   barely matters (0 vs 3.0 changes net by low single-digit percent, sign inconsistent
   across configs) - the reversal's edge lives in getting the STOP right, not the
   target. reverse_after_be=True (also reversing after a scratched breakeven stop, not
   only a true initial-stop loss) modestly beats reverse_after_be=False on every row -
   more reversal opportunities, same shape.
5. PLATEAU: NOT PLATEAU. Neighbours of the best config (rev/be=False/sf=1.5/tR=0 at 2.60
   MAR, rev/be=True/sf=0/tR=0 at 2.04, rev/be=True/sf=2.0/tR=0 at 2.48, rev/be=True/
   sf=1.5/tR=3.0 at 2.65) all sit BELOW the crown's 2.79 too, so the "keep >=70% of
   gain" check is moot (gain is negative - the best config never beats the crown, so
   there is no positive gain to test a plateau against). Printed as NOT PLATEAU by the
   driver's own definition (gain<=0 => keep=None => fail), which is the honest read:
   this fork does not beat the crown anywhere in the swept space, plateaued or not.

BOTTOM LINE: the trap trade is a real, modestly profitable pattern in isolation (PF up
to 1.25 on its own) but STACKING it onto the #314 crown as an intraday-second-trade adds
risk faster than it adds risk-adjusted return - every config FAILS G1 and most FAIL G3.
Not adopted this round. If revisited, the standalone reversal-only edge (bullet 3) is
the more promising thread than trying to bolt it onto the SAME day's already-completed
trade - e.g. as its own independent book/leg rather than a same-session add-on.
