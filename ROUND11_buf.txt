- ORB_3_6.py has no per-BAR ATR series; the closest thing it already computes for
  atr_filter is a per-SESSION quantity: the trailing-60-session MEDIAN session range,
  fixed before the session starts. This fork reuses that exact quantity, computed
  identically and independently of whether atr_filter itself is on, as the "ATR" for
  buf_mode=atr/hybrid and for the diagnostic below. It is a session-level, not bar-level,
  yardstick - stated here as the interpretation of "the same ATR series the parent
  already computes."
- buf_mode="atr": the buffer is buf_atr_mult x ATR, ignoring breakout_buf entirely.
  buf_mode="hybrid": buffer = max(breakout_buf x range, buf_atr_mult x ATR).
- Warm-up sessions (first 6) have no ATR reference; buf_mode=atr/hybrid falls back to
  the range-based buffer for those sessions only (a handful of trades in 16 years).
- confirm_bars / confirm_close_beyond are read ONLY inside the close_confirm=True path;
  with close_confirm=False (touch entry) they are never referenced - verified INERT by
  the smoke test (n and $ identical between confirm_bars=1/False and confirm_bars=3/True
  under close_confirm=False: n 2562 vs 2562, pnl $16,748.375 vs $16,748.375 in points).
- confirm_close_beyond, when confirm_bars>1: each direction's streak requires the new
  confirming close to clear the PREVIOUS confirming close in that direction (long: higher
  close each time; short: lower close each time). A close that fails the monotone test
  restarts the streak at 1 if it still clears the level, else resets to 0.
- Skipped grid cells (per the spec's "skip meaningless combinations"): buf_atr_mult swept
  only under buf_mode=atr/hybrid (buf_mode=range never reads buf_atr_mult - verified: it
  is bit-identical to the crown when buf_atr_mult=0, and buf_mode=range ignores the knob
  entirely regardless of its value); confirm_close_beyond swept only when confirm_bars>1
