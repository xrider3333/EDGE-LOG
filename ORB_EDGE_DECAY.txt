READ
1. The ORB edge is NOT decaying. On the crown it roughly doubles: profit factor 1.09 in the first
   half of its history to 1.41 in the second (permutation p 0.03), win rate 42.4% to 46.8%.
2. It is not a few fat winners. Strip the five biggest winners out of EVERY block and the rise
   survives: 0.86 to 1.11, p 0.05. That is the per-stretch concentration test, run because an
   ENGU-Q "recency pass" was retracted this week for failing exactly this.
3. It is not us picking it. The control repeats the test on configs never selected on recent data:
   the untouched parent default, a version with BOTH filters switched off, a deliberately wide
   stop, and two other opening-range lengths. Five of the six rise, and the strongest are the
   UNSELECTED ones (no filters at all, p 0.02; or_bars 3, p 0.00). A selection artefact would not
   show up in a config nobody ever chose.
4. It is measured dollar-free on purpose. Profit factor and win rate, never net per year - the
   index amplitude grew about sevenfold across this window and would manufacture a fake trend.
5. WHAT IT MEANS. Full-window statistics UNDERSTATE what this strategy is now. Gates written on
   full-window net (round 10's G2, for instance) are biased toward old-regime behaviour, and any
   candidate that wins 2010-2019 while losing 2020-2026 is being rewarded for the wrong half.
   It also explains, independently and on a different strategy family, the same pattern NOISE hit
   in its rounds 43-46: everything tuned on the old tape lost on the new one.
