QUESTION (plain words). Every lane judges a candidate book on the same two bars: walk-forward 98.5 %/yr
at a $30k drawdown with Sortino 3.816, and the lockbox year 155.54 with Sortino 4.150, where the lockbox
is BOOK #463's own 2025-06-30 .. 2026-06-30. That year has now been printed against for many candidates
(round 58's ~71 books, round 61's 18, the round-62 sizing rules, the Q1-Q6 queue, the crash-short and
rebalancing cells ...). The lockbox clause is close to a coin flip for a candidate with no edge (RESEARCH.md
finding 4: a no-edge series passes a 12-36 month sign test 44-53% of the time; of the ten one-change reads in
BOOK.md 10n/10p/10r, 3 cleared the full LB clause). So: after K looks, how likely is it that a no-edge
candidate has ALREADY cleared today's lockbox bar somewhere in the pile, and by what MARGIN over #463 must
a future candidate beat the lockbox for that chance to be under 5%? RESEARCH.md finding 7 and section 3e
("counting trials per FAMILY is still the test that matters, and it is still open") name this as the
missing input to every correction the engine makes. MDL r1 (ledger 2.51) says what a leg must EARN; this
round says how SURE a pass must be. It is an accounting round: no candidate is proposed, no sealed row of
any candidate is read, and its product is a number and a rule, not a discovery.

REFERENCE (unified convention, BOOK.md 10r; = r11_risk.py P2_REF). #463 WF 2016-07-01..2025-06-29:
ROC@$30k 93.81 / Sortino 3.816 / DD $44,849. LB 2025-06-30..2026-06-30 inclusive: 155.54 / 4.150 /
DD $49,855. Today's bar: WF ROC >= 98.50 AND WF Sortino >= 3.816 AND LB ROC >= 155.54 AND LB Sortino >=
4.150. Statistics are r11_risk.stats / unified verbatim: x = valued-daily P&L per index row (zeros
included); ROC = 30 x (net / years) / max drawdown, drawdown from a peak that starts at 0, years = (last
row - first row) / 365.25; Sortino = mean(x) / sqrt(mean(min(x, 0)^2)) x sqrt(252); CDR = 30 x (net /
years) / CDaR95 (report only). The WF clause is where candidates were CHOSEN, so a WF pass is a selection
event, not a test; the family-wise question is asked of the LB clause, with the WF analogue reported.

STEP 3 - TWO NULLS, NO CANDIDATE, NO EDGE (box). Every draw keeps #463's expected return in each
stretch and changes only what a no-information candidate of that type would change.
 N-LEG (the stand-in for a seat swap, a leg add or a combination): for leg i in {ORB, ENGU-Q, TTM, NOISE},
   B = 10,000 draws per leg (40,000 in all), numpy default_rng(20261004 + i): within the WF rows and
   within the LB rows SEPARATELY, draw r11_risk.stationary_bootstrap(n_rows, block=21, rng) over leg i's
   own valued-daily rows (Am_leg[i].T @ ones restricted to the stretch) and place the resampled rows on
   the stretch's rows in order; null book = the other three legs' original rows + the resampled leg.
   Score unified WF and LB (ROC, Sortino; CDR reported). Resampling breaks the leg's day alignment with
   the other legs, so N-LEG is CENTRED: a draw's LB excess is e = ln(ROC_LB,draw) - ln(median ROC_LB over
   that leg's draws), likewise for WF and for Sortino; a draw passes the LB clause at margin g iff
   e >= ln(1 + g) AND (Sortino_draw - median Sortino_draw) >= 0. The pooled N-LEG set = the four legs'
   draws with equal weight. The median shift of each leg (median ROC_LB,draw vs 155.54) is REPORTED; it is
   the alignment artefact the centring removes, not a stop.
 N-SIZE (the stand-in for a sizing rule; RISK r1's Null A read on the lockbox this time): V2 multipliers
   = augur_engine.book_sizing.vt_multipliers on the raw book over the FULL rule-U index 2010-06-07 ..
   2026-06-30 (lookback 20, reference 250, clip 0.5-2.0, 0.1 steps); every circular shift k in
   [250, N - 250] of the multiplier rows (about 3,680 shifts, deterministic, no seed), each applied
   trade by trade through Book.sized(m) (every daily mark re-sized by its entry row). UNCENTRED: a
   shifted V2 is a genuine no-information member of the sizing family, so its pass at today's bar is read
   as printed. V2-500 and V2-60 shifts are run and reported, not pooled. Disclosure: the lockbox year is
   spent for V2's family (BOOK.md 10o printed it; RISK r1 closed its backtest case); these shifts are
   nulls and no shift may be proposed as a candidate.
 Also, per draw where trade identity exists: LB_x = LB with the top trade removed (N-SIZE: the sized
   book's largest LB closed trade; N-LEG for the three legs other than ENGU-Q: the ENGU-Q 2026-04-07 ..
   05-12 trade's rows removed from the fixed ENGU-Q leg and from the reference; not computed for the
   ENGU-Q-seat draws and said so).

STEP 4 - THE NUMBERS (box). Margin grid g = 0, 0.005, ..., 1.000 (ratio units: the LB clause becomes
LB ROC >= 155.54 x (1 + g), the Sortino clause unchanged).
 p_leg(g) = share of pooled N-LEG draws passing the LB clause at g; p_size(g) = share of N-SIZE shifts.
 FWER(g) = 1 - (1 - p_leg(g))^K_leg x (1 - p_size(g))^K_size.
 Reported: p_leg(0), p_size(0), FWER(0) = "how likely a no-edge candidate has already cleared today's
 bar"; g* = the smallest grid g with FWER(g) <= 0.05, with the g at FWER 0.025 and 0.10 as its band; the
 per-leg g* for each N-LEG seat; g*_x on LB_x; g*_CDR with CDR in place of ROC; the WF analogue (p_WF(0)
 under both nulls and the WF margin at FWER 0.05), report only; the margin ladder g*(K) at K = 1, 10, 50,
 100, K_book, 2 x K_book, 500 (what a future read costs). Resolution: FWER <= 0.05 at K_leg ~ 156 needs
 p_leg <= 0.00033 = about 13 of 40,000 draws, and p_size <= 0.0051 at K_size ~ 10 = about 19 of 3,680
 shifts; plain counts, no smoothing; the harness prints the counts beside every share.
 CALIBRATION (registered scale, never a stop): the ten real one-change reads on #463 in BOOK.md
 10n / 10p / 10r (#468, #469, #470, Q1a, Q1b, Q2, Q2ctx, Q3, Q5, Q6; each as its printed LB ROC over
 #463's LB ROC in the same table, from the step-1 csv) give sd_real = sd of ln(LB ratio); sd_null = sd of
 the pooled N-LEG centred LB excess e; r = sd_real / sd_null. ADJUSTED MARGIN g_adj = g* x max(1, r): the
 null's spread is scaled UP to the real swaps' when they are noisier, never down. r is printed; r outside
 [0.5, 2.0] is flagged as "the null does not describe the real swaps' spread" beside g_adj.

 58d; #444; #449 (the adoption read that became #463); the round-61 best; V2 and V2-500 (10o); Q4 and Q6

 THE RULE THIS ROUND PROPOSES (an owner decision via MANAGER, never automatic): for any BACKTEST
 candidate judged on the spent lockbox year from now on, the LB clause reads LB ROC >= 155.54 x (1 +
 g_adj) with Sortino >= 4.150 unchanged (or the ex-top-trade form if g*_x < g*), and a candidate that

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED. The nulls are stand-ins for "a candidate of that type with no information", not
for any particular candidate; a real swap carries its own return and its own drawdown timing, which is
exactly why the calibration scale r exists and why MDL r1's map, not this round, says what a leg must
earn. K counts printed looks; unprinted scans that were run and discarded are not counted, so K is a
floor and FWER(0) a lower bound. One realised history: the block-21 bootstrap keeps three-week
structure, not regimes; the N-SIZE shifts keep everything but the timing.

and are the only lockbox rows touched. Seeds, block, grid, K-rule, calibration set and the past-pass
list are fixed here; the csv is hashed before any draw. The round reads no sealed row of any candidate
and proposes bar changes for FUTURE reads only.
