WHAT IS DIFFERENT (one line): the first EL family whose signal is a decision of the BOARD - the size of the regular dividend it

WHY NOW (house evidence first). The 2026-10-06 finding line: short-growth baskets earn in #463's drawdowns and pay for it in
the 2019-21 growth rally; the matched-drawdown yardstick rejects them. Here every name, long or short, is a dividend payer, so

THE MAP FIRST (MDL r1). A dollar-neutral basket placed as a standalone leg first and a drawdown-set candidate second.
Standalone bar: ROC @ $30k >= 15. Placed honestly: the classic evidence is for INITIATIONS (+7.5% the next year) and OMISSIONS
(-11%) (Michaely, Thaler & Womack 1995), with smaller drift after ordinary increases and decreases (Yoon & Starks 1995;
Grullon, Michaely & Swaminathan 2002 find little); in the 500 most liquid names 2016-25 a few percent a year is the honest
range - at $200,000 a side ~$4,000-10,000 a year against a drawdown of perhaps $20,000-40,000 (ROC 2-10). Its drawdown-day
case: raisers are cash-rich, low-leverage firms and cutters are stressed ones, so the spread should widen in sell-offs - but
a cut is known only at its ex-date, AFTER the stock has fallen, and 2020's cutters (energy, banks, REITs) led the 2020-11 ..
2021 rebound. That bleed is this family's version of the 2019-21 risk and is printed first.

MECHANISM. Boards smooth dividends and change them only when they believe the change is sustainable (Lintner 1956), so a
change signals management's view; investors underreact to it (Michaely et al. 1995; Benartzi, Michaely & Thaler 1997 on the
earnings that follow); dividend payers that keep raising are the 'quality' firms whose returns survive risk adjustment
(Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen 2019). Counter-evidence written in now: (1) much of the drift is pre-2000 and in small caps; (2)
the declaration date is not in the calendar, so the signal waits for the EX-DATE (weeks after the news); (3) cuts cluster
in one crisis (2020); (4) the calendar starts 2016-06-01, so a 12-month change exists from about 2017-06 and an initiation
(24 months without a dividend) from about 2018-06.

[S] THE SCORE, known at a rank close r (only calendar rows with ex_date <= r; the amount is public before the ex-date, so this
is late, never early). For each universe name: its REGULAR dividends = cash rows with special = False and rate > 0, on the
split-adjusted basis D* = rate / F(ex-date) (RESMOM's [R1] convention). Its FREQUENCY = the median spacing of its last four
regular ex-dates (monthly 20-40 days, quarterly 60-120, semiannual 150-210, annual 330-400; anything else = no score,
counted). D*_0 = the latest regular dividend (ex-date e0 <= r); D*_1 = the regular dividend whose ex-date is nearest to
e0 - 365 days within +-45 days. G = ln(D*_0 / D*_1). SUSPENDED: a payer whose e0 is older than 1.75 x its frequency at r ->
G = -10 (the bottom). INITIATOR: a first regular dividend within the last 365 days after at least 730 days with none (the
calendar must cover those 730 days) -> G = +10 (the top). No regular dividend in the last 730 days = a NON-PAYER: no score,
never traded. A frequency change between D*_1 and D*_0, or |G| > ln(4) without a split or spin-off between them (a data
error), = no score (listed by name).
