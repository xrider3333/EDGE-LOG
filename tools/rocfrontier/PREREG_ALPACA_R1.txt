FAMILY A - SIPORB r1: 5-minute opening-range breakout on the day's 20 "stocks in play" (Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz 2024,
"A Profitable Day Trading Strategy For The U.S. Equity Market", SSRN 4729284 - rules as printed in the paper)
Mechanism: stocks with news trade at many times their normal opening volume; that flow keeps going in the direction of
the first five minutes, and the move is stock-specific, so it should be nearly uncorrelated with the NQ/ES book.
- Universe each day: US-listed common stocks (Alpaca assets, status active AND inactive, so delisted names are included
  where Alpaca has their bars) with (1) opening price > $5, (2) average volume over the previous 14 sessions >= 1,000,000
  shares, (3) ATR(14) over the previous 14 sessions > $0.50, (4) Relative Volume >= 100%, where Relative Volume = the
  09:30-09:35 ET volume / the average 09:30-09:35 volume of the previous 14 sessions. Filters 1-3 use RAW (unadjusted)
  daily bars as they stood that day; a split inside the 14-session look-back excludes the name that day.
- Selection: the 20 highest Relative Volume names (fewer if fewer pass).
- Direction by the first 5-minute bar: close > open -> buy stop at its high; close < open -> sell stop at its low;
  close = open -> no order. The stop order lives until 15:59 ET. Fill = the stop price, or the bar's open if a 1-minute
  bar opens through it.
- Protective stop: 10% of ATR(14) from the fill price. Exit: the close of the 15:59 1-minute bar if not stopped.
- Size (fixed, no compounding, on $100k): 20 slots of $5,000; shares = min(1% of the slot / stop distance,
  4 x the slot / entry price), rounded down.
- Costs: $0.0035 a share per side (the paper's) + $0.01 a share per side slippage; stress = $0.02 slippage.
- Cells: A1 both sides (PRIMARY, the paper's design). Reported, never a pass route: A2 long side only; the RAW TWIN
  (the same 5-minute ORB on every filtered name without the Relative Volume filter or top-20 cut - the paper's
  base strategy).
- Stretches: REPLICATION = 2016-01-04 -> 2023-12-29 (the paper's own sample - NOT out-of-sample; it checks the pipeline:
  if A1's replication Sharpe is below 1.0 against the paper's 2.81, the data or code is not reproducing the paper and
  Stage A is not judged until that gap is explained). WF = 2024-01-02 -> 2025-06-29 (after the paper; the real test).
- STAGE A (WF): >= 100 trades; ROC @ $30k >= 15; profit factor >= 1.10; t-stat of daily P&L >= 2.0; beats the RAW
  TWIN on ROC @ $30k AND Sortino; net > 0 at the stress cost; profitable without its single biggest trade; >= 60% of
  months positive.
- STAGE A2 (WF): EITHER standalone ROC @ $30k >= #463's 187.79 on the same stretch, OR BOOK #463 + this leg (weight
  c in {0.5, 1, 2} x the sizing above, the best c by WF book ROC @ $30k, then frozen) >= 1.05 x 187.79 = 197.2 with
  Sortino >= 4.428.
- STAGE B (LB, once): standalone LB ROC @ $30k >= 164.76 with >= 50 LB trades and profitable without its biggest trade,
  OR the frozen book add beats #463's LB (>= 164.76 and Sortino >= 4.150, LB profitable without its biggest trade).
- STAGE C: the engine has no multi-stock job type, so a pass becomes a written FORWARD PAPER SHADOW (Webull paper) with
  its own bar + an owner call; no Auto-Validate applies.
- Known ways it could fool us: survivorship (Alpaca may lack some delisted names - the replication check and a count
  of names passing filters 1-3 per year are reported); borrow for shorts is assumed; stop fills in fast markets.

FAMILY B - NQBRD r1: an NQ trend-day trigger from Nasdaq-100 breadth at 10:00 ET
Mechanism: when most of the index's stocks are already above their own open by 10:00, the buying is broad
(institutional), not one mega-cap - the trend should persist into the close. The index tape alone cannot see it.
- Members: point-in-time Nasdaq-100 membership file tools/data/ndx_members.csv, built from Nasdaq's annual
  reconstitution announcements and the public change log BEFORE any bar is read; removed members count for their
  membership period.
- Breadth B = share of that day's members (with bars) whose 09:55-bar close is above their 09:30 open (5-minute bars,
  raw - a same-day ratio).
- Trade NQ (5m RTH, roll-corrected master, 1 contract, cost 0.533): B >= theta -> long at the open of the 10:00 bar;
  B <= 1 - theta -> short; exit at the close of the 15:55 bar. No stop.
- Cells: theta in {0.70, 0.80} (PRIMARY). Control, never a pass route: NQ's own 09:30-10:00 direction on the same number
  of days per year, picked by the largest |09:30-10:00 return|. Null: each traded day's direction replaced by a fair coin,
  500 replicates, statistic = the max WF t over the 2 cells.
- WF = 2016-07-01 -> 2025-06-29.
- STAGE A (WF): >= 100 trades; ROC @ $30k >= 15; profit factor >= 1.10; t >= 2.0 AND above the null's 95th percentile;
  beats the control on ROC @ $30k; net > 0 at +0.25 pt; profitable without its single biggest trade; >= 60% of years
  positive.
- STAGE A2 (WF): BOOK #463 + this leg at c in {1, 2, 3} contracts (best c by WF book ROC @ $30k, then frozen)
  >= 1.05 x 92.70 = 97.3 with Sortino >= 3.816.
- STAGE B (LB, once): book LB ROC @ $30k >= 164.76, Sortino >= 4.150, book LB profitable without its biggest trade,
  the leg's own LB net > 0.
- STAGE C: a strategy file reading a frozen daily breadth file + ONE Auto-Validate at the 900-trial budget (theta as the
  open range, pinned window, same lockbox), then the RUNBOARD watch list and MANAGER. Owner decides.
- Known ways it could fool us: breadth days are NOISE's trend days (the book-add stage prices that); membership errors.

Zero passes at any stage = that family dead; ledger + memory.
