Not a duplicate: the NOISE (round 60) and ORB (round 62) lanes use this calendar only to SIZE their RTH trades in
the reaction session. This family is a standalone overnight position taken at the evening reopen.

Mechanism: the seven largest Nasdaq-100 weights report just after 16:00 ET; NQ prices the shock in the thin
16:00-17:00 ET futures session. The house pattern (continuation beats reversion) predicts that the move keeps
going when Europe and the US cash open re-price a large slice of the index.

- Events: tools/data/megacap_earnings.csv (SEC 8-K Item 2.02, AAPL MSFT NVDA AMZN GOOGL META TSLA, 461 releases),
  accepted 16:00-16:59 ET on a day with an NQ session; several releases the same evening = one event.
- P0 = close of the last bar starting before 16:00 ET that day; P1 = close of the last bar starting before 17:00
  ET. m = (P1 - P0) / ATR, ATR = mean high-low of the 20 complete 18:00->17:00 ET sessions ending that day.
- Decide at 17:00 ET (the close of the 16:55 bar); enter at the open of the next session's first bar (18:00 ET)
  in the direction of m (continuation). No stop.
- Cells: {NQ (primary), ES} x k in {0, 0.1, 0.2} (trade only if |m| >= k) x X in {next session 09:25 ET open,
  next session 16:00 ET = close of the 15:55 bar} = 12 primary cells. FADE mirrors are controls only.
- Null: each event's direction replaced by a fair coin, 500 replicates, statistic = MAX WF per-trade t over the
  12 primary cells. Stage A d: t >= 2.0 AND above the null's 95th percentile.
