WHY THIS FAMILY (the map first). MDL r1: a leg that earns in #463's drawdown weeks lifts the book at almost any positive
return (rho_dd <= -0.15: half the time from a standalone ROC @ $30k of 5-10; <= -0.45: from ~0); everything else needs ~$15k
a year at a $30k drawdown of its own and low rho_dd. #463's falling weeks are of two kinds: STRESS weeks (2020-02..04,
2022, 2025-04: volatility spikes, two-way crashes) and quiet grinds (2025-05..06: breakouts fail). This family targets the
stress weeks only and stays near flat in the quiet ones.

crash-regime short died 18/18 and DIP (buying those days) is a profitable family, so such days revert on average; an

else; cross-asset trend (crisis alpha) - TREND r1 (2026-09-24): real in 2008 / 2020 / 2022, too thin between crises.

MECHANISM. When volatility spikes, liquidity providers pull back, forced and liquidity-driven trading pushes prices past
fair value, and the move reverses; the expected return to supplying liquidity - short-term reversal - rises with market
volatility (Nagel 2012, RFS "Evaporating Liquidity"; Grossman & Miller 1988; Lehmann 1990; Jegadeesh 1990). Moves WITHOUT
news reverse, moves WITH news drift (Chan 2003, JFE) - so both legs fade only where there is no sign of news. Both legs are
dollar-neutral with BOTH sides always filled [T1], so their P&L is not the book's market beta; the realised beta to ES in
#463's drawdown weeks is reported FIRST [M2] (long losers = long high beta in a crash - the test must see it). This is the
mirror image of SIPORB (news-driven moves continue). The literature's own warning is built in: a reversal measured and
traded at the same print earns the bid-ask bounce, not liquidity provision [O1] - neither leg does that (L1, L2 below).

DATA HYGIENE [T2] (before any ranking): a name is excluded from a position, and from the null's pool for that rebalance, when
any of these falls on a session from 5 sessions before its ranking window through its exit: a registered split (SIPORB's
split factor moved > 1%), a gap-scan flag (raw open / prior raw close more than 25% from 1 and within 2% of a whole ratio k or
1/k, k <= 50), a TBIS volume-confirmed flag (C:\EdgeLog\_research_cache\split_qa\siporb_split_flags_voltest.csv), or a raw
overnight gap beyond +-50% with no split-factor change (spin-off / bad print). Counts reported; the 20 largest name-period
gains are listed with their dates so a reader can see none is a data event. Flags that fall AFTER the decision (inside the
hold) are a look-ahead removal - they drop exactly the positions that jumped, in both directions - so every result is also
reported with those positions kept at their naive raw P&L; the verdict reads the registered removal, and a flip between the
two readings is said [O3].
LARGE-MOVE CROSS-CHECK [O3 (i)]: every position whose L1 signal return is beyond +-25% or whose L2 raw gap is beyond +-20% is
checked three ways - the raw and split-adjusted series agree over the window (no factor change), the move is not a whole
ratio (the gap scan above), and the raw share volume of the move's session does not jump by about the inverse of the price
ratio (TBIS's volume test: for L1 the move's session against the 20 sessions before it, inside the ranking window; for L2 the
09:30-09:35 volume against its 14-session mean, known at 09:35). Per year: the count checked and the count that disagree; a
disagreement removes the position from the cell and the null.

UNIVERSE, each rebalance (only information known before the decision [M6]): US common stocks in SIPORB's asset list passing
its symbol / name filters; prior raw close >= $10; the 500 largest by mean dollar volume (raw close x volume) over the
previous 20 sessions, all 20 present; SIPORB's filters 2-3 on the previous 14 sessions (mean volume >= 1,000,000 shares, ATR
> $0.50); no registered split in the 14 sessions before (splits are announced in advance). For L2 this set is chosen before
09:30 and is a subset of the names SIPORB's open5 pull ASKED (asked = names passing SIPORB filters 1-3 on any of sessions t ..
t+14; filter 1, opening price > $5, is implied by a prior close >= $10 except on a gap beyond -50%, where the name then has no
09:30 bar and is counted, not replaced). NEWS proxy (known before the decision): a session whose volume is >= 3x the name's
previous 20-session mean (daily bars); for L2 on day t also a 09:30-09:35 Relative Volume >= 2 (SIPORB's measure).

L1 WEEKLY REVERSAL [M1, T4, O1, O2]. RANK on each week's last session close: the split-safe 5-session return through that
close minus the universe's mean; exclude names in the news on any of those 5 sessions - all five are complete before the
fill session, so no volume the trade could not have seen is read (ORB B2 = MANAGER edit 1). FILL at the NEXT session's
official opening print (usually Monday's open): the print that makes the signal (the rank close) is never the fill price -
the skip that removes bid-ask bounce from a reversal test (ORB B1). Long the 25 lowest, short the 25 highest, $4,000 each
(equal dollars, both sides always 25 - the universe has 500 names). HOLD to the next rebalance's fill (the following week's
first opening print). Positions are valued at every session close from the daily bars (and the fill / exit from their opening
prints), so drawdown, DO and rho_dd are daily quantities, never weekly closed P&L [O8].
L2 NO-NEWS GAP FADE [T1, O1]. On each session t, among the universe names with a 09:30-09:35 bar and Relative Volume < 2: raw
gap = 09:35 price (close of that bar) / prior raw close (split-safe) - 1; ADJUSTED gap = raw gap - beta x ES's gap, ES's gap =
(ES 09:35 price - ES prior 16:00 close) / that close (roll-corrected changes, unadjusted level), beta = the OLS slope of the
name's daily close-to-close returns on ES's over the previous 60 sessions (all present). Long the 20 most negative adjusted
gaps, short the 20 most positive, only |adjusted gap| >= 1%; the session trades ONLY if both sides fill all 20; $5,000 each.
FILL: open5 holds only the first 5-minute bar, so the signal print is the only print - each name fills at the 09:35 price
PLUS an adverse 10 bps (a buy 10 bps above it, a sell 10 bps below), 20 bps as the stress, and the cell must pass at both
(MANAGER #43 (a)); a 1-minute next-bar fill was rejected because min1 bars cover only SIPORB's top names (a selection). Exit
at the official close of t. Reported per traded day: net dollar exposure and its beta to ES.
STRESS LEAN [M6, O9] (cells "S"): every position x k_t; k_t = RV20_t / median(RV20 over the previous 252 sessions), clipped to
[0.5, 2.0]; RV20_t = the standard deviation of ES's last 20 daily returns (sessions t-20 .. t-1), a daily return = (roll-
corrected 16:00 price change) / prior unadjusted 16:00 price; known at the prior close. The constants (20 sessions, 252,
the clip) are fixed from the literature's convention before any number, not fitted to 2020 / 2022 / 2025; the ES masters
start in 2010, so k_t is defined for every session from 2016 on. Cells "F": k_t = 1.
CELLS: L1-F, L1-S, L2-F, L2-S (the family: 4 cells).
