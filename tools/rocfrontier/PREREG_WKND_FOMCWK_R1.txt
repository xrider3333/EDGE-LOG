COMMON RULES
- Data: roll-corrected masters only (house ADJ, registry ids NQ 5m ETH 71, ES 5m ETH 62, NQ 5m RTH 73,
  ES 5m RTH 63). Candidate data is sliced to bars BEFORE 2025-06-30 before any signal is computed; the lockbox
  (2025-06-30 -> 2026-06-30, BOOK #463's) is read only by the Stage B script, only for Stage A2 passes.
- Clock US/Eastern. Bar timestamps are bar starts; a bar's close is known at start + 5 minutes. Signals are
  decided at a bar's close and filled at the next bar's open. Calendars are known in advance.
- One contract per trade in Stage A. Costs: NQ 0.533 pts, ES 0.363 pts per round trip (house); stress +0.25 pt.
- Stretches: EARLY = first bar with warm-up -> 2016-06-30 (sign check). WF = 2016-07-01 -> 2025-06-29.
- Yardstick (owner rule 2026-09-28): ROC %/yr at a $30,000 worst drawdown valued daily = 30 x MAR.

FAMILY W - WKND r1 (family OVERNIGHT): trade the weekend gap on the Sunday reopen
Mechanism: weekend news is first priced in thin Sunday-evening liquidity; the house pattern (intraday
continuation beats reversion) predicts the rest of the move is finished by Europe and the US open.
- F = close of the last bar starting before 17:00 ET on the last session before the weekend.
- S = open of the first bar of the new week (starting at/after 18:00 ET Sunday).
- ATR = mean high-low of the 20 previous complete 18:00->17:00 ET sessions, known at F. g = (S - F) / ATR.
- Decide at the close of the week's first bar; enter at the next bar's open in the direction of g
  (continuation). No stop. Exit at the open of the first bar at/after X.
- Cells: {NQ, ES} x k in {0, 0.25, 0.5} (trade only if |g| >= k; g = 0 never trades) x
  X in {Monday 03:00 ET, Monday 09:25 ET, Monday 16:00 ET (close of the 15:55 bar)} = 18 primary cells.
  Controls, never a pass route: the 18 FADE mirrors.
- A week is skipped if a true roll switch (tools/data/rolls_<ROOT>.csv, kind != not_a_roll) falls between F
  and the exit (calendar rule - never a jump-size rule, the ONDRIFT trap).
- Family-wide null: each week's direction replaced by a fair coin, all 18 cells re-scored, 500 replicates;
  the statistic is the MAX over the 18 cells of the WF per-trade t-stat.

FAMILY F - FOMCWK r1 (family MISC): the FOMC-cycle even weeks (Cieslak, Morse & Vissing-Jorgensen, JF 2019)
Mechanism: Fed-driven news flow clusters in even weeks of the FOMC cycle, so the equity premium is earned there.
- FOMC days: tools/data/fomc_dates.txt (scheduled decision days only, from federalreserve.gov).
- Trading days = the master's RTH session dates; daily close = close of the last bar starting before 16:00 ET.
- Cycle day d(t) = trading days since the latest FOMC day <= t; the trading day just before an FOMC day is
  d = -1 of that next cycle. Week 0 = d in [-1, 3], 1 = [4, 8], 2 = [9, 13], 3 = [14, 18], 4 = [19, 23],
  5 = [24, 28], 6 = [29, 33]; d >= 34 = week 7 (odd).
- Leg: long on even-week days (0, 2, 4, 6): enter at the daily close before each even block, exit at the close of
  its last day; one round-trip cost per block. Valued daily at each close.
- Cells: ES (primary), NQ (primary). Controls, never a pass route: odd weeks, always-long, week 0 only.
- Null: every cycle's week labels shifted by a random 0-9 trading days, 500 replicates; statistic = the WF
  annualised Sharpe of daily P&L.

STAGE A - a cell passes only if ALL hold on WF:
