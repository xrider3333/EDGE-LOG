MECHANISM. Retail investors buy what grabs their attention: individuals are net BUYERS of attention-grabbing stocks after
up AND down news, because buying needs a search and selling only covers what they already own (Barber & Odean 2008,
RFS; abnormal volume is one of their attention measures). Their orders, placed after the close and overnight, execute at
the next open, so high-attention stocks earn a premium from the close to the next open that reverses during the next
day (Berkman, Koch, Tuttle & Zhang 2012, JFQA, "Paying Attention: Overnight Returns and the Hidden Cost of Buying at the
Open"; Lou, Polk & Skouras 2019, JFE; Aboody, Even-Tov, Lehavy & Trueman 2018, JFQA). Those samples end 2008-2014; the
walk-forward here (2016-2025) is after them and covers the zero-commission retail boom (2019 on). The premium is
stock-specific and the basket is hedged with NQ while the book is flat overnight except its ENGU-Q leg, so the leg
should be close to uncorrelated with #463.

RULES
- Attention set, session t: SIPORB's "stocks in play" EXACTLY as PREREG_ALPACA_R1.txt family A defines them (filters
  1-3 on raw daily bars as they stood, the split-window exclusion, Relative Volume >= 100% on the 09:30-09:35 bar, the
  20 highest Relative Volume, ties by symbol; r5_siporb.py Data.top20 is the implementation), known at 09:35 ET on t.
  Every selected name is in the set whatever its first 5-minute bar did (the mechanism is buying after up AND down
  news). Then dropped, not replaced: a name whose 09:35 price (close of its 09:30-09:35 bar) is under $5, or whose
  average daily dollar volume (raw close x volume, mean of the previous 14 sessions) is under $5m. No rule reads
  session t after 09:35, or anything of session t+1.
- Trade: buy each name at session t's closing-auction print (Alpaca daily raw close); sell at the next session's
  opening-auction print (raw open of t+1). A Friday-to-Monday or holiday night is one night. Shares = floor($5,000 /
  close_t); at most 20 x $5,000 = $100k gross long a night, fixed (no compounding). CAPACITY: $100k a night per side
  (long stocks, short NQ) - a paper-book scale, stated as such.
- Split-safe P&L: shares x (open_t+1 x F_t / F_t+1 - close_t), F = raw open / split-adjusted open (SIPORB's split
  factor, known at each open).
- Hedge (cell H): sell NQ at session t's 16:00 ET print (close of the 15:55 bar; 13:00 = close of the 12:55 bar on NYSE
  half days) and buy it back at the 09:30 print of t+1 (open of the 09:30 bar); contracts = the basket's cost at the
  close / (unadjusted NQ 16:00 price x $20), fractional; P&L from the roll-corrected master's change. Whole-MNQ rounding
  is reported, not judged. Cell U: no hedge.
- Exit that cannot be priced (no bar at t+1: halt, delisting): the first raw open within the next 10 sessions,
  split-safe; none within 10 sessions = a loss of 100% of the position (conservative), counted; 0% reported beside it.
- GAP SCAN (the killer risk - an unadjusted split prints a fake overnight move, GE's 1-for-8): BEFORE any number, every
  symbol-day of the universe gets R = raw open / prior session's raw close; it is FLAGGED when R is finite, |R - 1| >
  0.25, the registered split rule (F moved > 1%) did not fire that day, and R is within 2% of a whole number k or of 1/k
  for some k in 2..50 (SIPORB's missed_split_flags). Every flagged symbol-day is REMOVED - the night ending at that open
  is not traded in either cell or in the random-pick null; the count is reported, by year (and, report only, the
  attention set's nights at the raw R).
- Dividends: no dividend calendar is on the machine; Alpaca's split adjustment keeps dividends in the price, so on an
  ex-date the long side loses the dividend at the open. The bias is AGAINST the strategy; stated, not corrected.
- Costs: 5 bps of the stock notional a side (base); stress 10 and 20 bps a side. Hedge: 0.533 NQ points a round trip
  per contract ($10.66), pro rata.

CELLS: H = hedged = the ONLY primary cell. U = unhedged = reported only, never a pass route, never swapped in.
NULL: 500 draws; in each, every night, the same number of names as that night's attention set drawn at random from the
same day's eligible universe (filters 1-3, the $5 / $5m exclusions, gap-scan removals), same sizing, hedge and costs;
seed 20261004. Statistic = the hedged basket's WF ROC @ $30k.
