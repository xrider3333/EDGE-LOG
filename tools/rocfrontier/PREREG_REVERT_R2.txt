MECHANISM. From 15:50 ET the NYSE and Nasdaq publish closing-auction (market-on-close) imbalances. Index-arbitrage and
hedging desks lean on the futures in 15:50-16:00 ahead of the closing flow, pushing them in the imbalance's direction.
The cash auction prints at 16:00; the futures trade on to 17:00, and once the auction is done those hedges are unwound, so
part of the 15:50-16:00 push should come back in the next quarter hour (temporary price impact reverts; e.g. the
closing-auction reversal documented for stocks by Bogousslavsky & Muravyev 2023). Prediction: the 16:00-16:15 futures
move is opposite the 15:50-16:00 move, more so when that move is large for the day.

RULES
- Data: the house roll-corrected 1-minute ETH masters (ADJ, source db_adj_eth; registry ids NQ 66, ES 58). They are
  back-adjusted by constant offsets, so intraday point P&L equals the traded contract's, and contract switches fall at
  19:00-20:00 ET, never inside 15:50-16:15. Bars are stamped at their START, clock US/Eastern. Every array is cut to bars

- Eligible session: a weekday with a full cash session (>= 300 one-minute bars starting 09:30-15:59; early closes are
  skipped), all 25 bars starting 15:50-16:14 present, and no mega-cap evening earnings release that day
  (tools/data/megacap_earnings_dates.csv, report time >= 16:00; the calendar is known in advance).
- Signal: m = close of the 15:59 bar - open of the 15:50 bar (the cash session's last ten minutes). Scale s = median of
  |m| over the previous 60 eligible sessions (at least 40 needed, else no trade). Trade if m != 0 and |m| >= k x s.
- Trade: the side OPPOSITE the sign of m, 1 contract, entry at the open of the 16:00 bar, exit at the close of the 16:14
  bar (16:15). No stop, nothing held past 16:15.
- Costs per round trip: NQ 0.533 pt ($20/pt), ES 0.363 pt ($50/pt) (house rule); stress +0.25 pt.
- Cells: {NQ, ES} x k in {0, 1, 2} = 6 primary cells. Control, never a pass route: the continuation mirror (same days,
  the side of m).
- Stretches: EARLY = first eligible session -> 2016-06-30 (sign check); WF = 2016-07-01 -> 2025-06-29.
- Family-wide null: on each market each eligible day's side is replaced by a fair coin (one coin per market-day, shared by
  that market's three k cells), cost re-applied; statistic = the MAX over the 6 cells of the WF per-trade t; 500 reps.
- Yardstick (owner rule 2026-09-28): ROC %/yr at a $30,000 worst drawdown of the daily P&L = 30 x MAR (every trade closes
  the same day); Sortino = mean / RMS of negative daily P&L x sqrt(252), zero days included.
