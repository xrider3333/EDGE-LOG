WHAT IS DIFFERENT (one line): the first EL family whose signal is a firm's AGE ON THE EXCHANGE - the new-issues puzzle (Ritter
1991; Loughran & Ritter 1995) - not a price pattern, a news event or a dividend date.

WHY NOW (house evidence first). RESMOM r1 (ledger 2.65) showed that a monthly, low-turnover stock basket can survive costs and
earn in #463's drawdown days; its biggest drawdown-day gains came in 2022, when the growth names #463's NQ legs trade fell
hardest. Recent listings are the purest growth-and-attention names in the market; the mechanism below says they underperform for
two years after the lockup, and a basket short them is the most direct test of whether that underperformance is a lasting
premium or one boom-and-bust (2020-22) - which is why the registered bars below include "net > 0 without 2022".

THE MAP FIRST (MDL r1). A dollar-neutral basket of tens of names a side; rho_dd unknown (the short side is growth-heavy, so the
drawdown-week profile could be strongly positive, like RESMOM's, or concentrated in one year). Standalone bar ROC @ $30k >= 15;
the MANAGER #70 route (gate, then one forward book line) applies if only the book-add report passes. Placed honestly: few names,
a lumpy cohort structure and one dominant episode - a long shot whose value would be its drawdown-day profile.

MECHANISM. Firms list when investors are most optimistic about them ("windows of opportunity"); the optimism fades as the story
meets results, and the supply of tradable shares jumps when lockups expire (~180 days; Field & Hanka 2001), so new issues
underperform seasoned firms of similar size for three to five years (Ritter 1991; Loughran & Ritter 1995; Ritter & Welch 2002).
Counter-evidence written in now: the underperformance is concentrated in small, non-VC-backed issues and shrinks when issues
are matched on size and book-to-market (Brav & Gompers 1997; Brav, Geczy & Gompers 2000) - this family trades only the 500 most
liquid names, where the effect should be weakest; borrowing recent issues can be costly; and 2016-25 holds one great listing
boom (2020-21) and its bust (2022), so any result may be that one episode.

DATA (no new pull, no money): the SIPORB cache (Alpaca SIP daily bars raw + split-adjusted, active and inactive names, manifest
sha256 380b05f2..., never mixed with a re-pull) - a name's LISTING SESSION is its first session with a bar in the cache; the wide
corporate-actions calendar pinned for RESMOM (sha256 e5bc8487...) for spin-offs, name changes, dividends (added back as RESMOM
[R1]) and splits; ES 5-minute RTH masters for the hedge cell. Hygiene exactly as RESMOM; a flag inside the hold removes the
position (look-ahead removal, reported both ways).

WHO IS NEW, WHO IS SEASONED (at each rank close r, information known then):
- NEW: a universe name whose listing session is 6 to 24 months before r (126 .. 504 sessions), and which is NOT a spin-off's new
  symbol on the calendar (spincos are a different effect, excluded and counted) and has NO name change on the calendar in the 24
  months before r (de-SPACs and rebrands cannot be dated cleanly; excluded and counted; their P&L had they been included is a
  REPORTED row).
- SEASONED: a universe name listed more than 36 months before r or present at the cache's first session (2016-01-04), with no name
  change in the 24 months before r.
- Because the cache starts 2016-01-04, a listing is dated only from 2016-01-05 on; the first rank whose 24-month window is fully
  observable is 2018-01-31, so WF = positions EXITED 2018-02-01 -> 2025-06-29 (89 rebalances; counted by the dryload).
UNIVERSE: RESMOM's (prior raw close >= $10, top 500 by mean raw dollar volume over the previous 20 sessions, mean volume >=
1,000,000 shares, ATR > $0.50, no registered split in the previous 14 sessions); no 252-session history is required of NEW names.

CELLS (the family: 2), at each month's last session close r, filled at the next session's official open, held to the next
rebalance's fill, $4,000 a name, fractional shares, daily marks, no compounding:
- M (matched): SHORT every NEW name in the universe; LONG, for each, the SEASONED universe name nearest to it in 20-session mean
  dollar volume (each seasoned name used once, ties by symbol). Dollar-neutral.
- E (ES-hedged): SHORT every NEW name; LONG ES at the short side's notional x 1.0 (a market hedge, not a beta estimate - new names
  lack the 252-session history). Market-neutral to first order.
A month with fewer than 10 NEW names trades nothing in either cell (counted).
COSTS: 5 bps a side on the stocks (base), stress 10 and 20; ES 0.5 bps a side. BORROW on the short side: 2%/yr base (recent
listings are often special), stress 10%/yr and 25%/yr on every short day.

NULL (family-aware): 500 draws, seed 20261005; at every rebalance the NEW set is replaced by the same number of SEASONED universe
names drawn at random (not NEW), and cell M's matched longs are re-matched to them by the same rule; statistic = the MAX over the 2
cells of WF ROC @ $30k. It asks whether being NEW matters beyond being a random liquid stock of the same count.

#463 and the map: exactly RESMOM's (reproduction refused unless WF 93.81 / 3.816 / $44,849 and LB 155.54 / 4.150; DO and rho_dd by
r12_mdl.Stretch; 28 episodes / 460 days - note WF here starts 2018-02, so the map point uses the drawdown days from 2018-02 on).

STAGE A (WF, per cell; the standalone bars decide - MANAGER #56): (a) >= 60 rebalances traded; (b) WF ROC @ $30k >= 15 and net > 0
at 5 AND 10 bps AND at the 10%/yr borrow stress; (c) above the null's 95th percentile; (d) positive in >= 5 of the 8 July-June WF
years (2017-18 is short and counts), net > 0 without Feb 15 - Apr 30 2020, AND net > 0 without calendar 2022 (the bust year);

asset list, the calendar - is it really a new listing?) before a pass is read.

REPORTED, never a pass route: NEW names per month and by listing cohort year; the P&L by cohort and by months since listing (6-12

KNOWN WAYS IT COULD FOOL US: the listing date is the first bar in one vendor's cache (uplistings from OTC, ticker reuse and
direct listings look like IPOs - the hand audit and the exclusions guard part of it); one boom-bust; borrow costs are guessed;
survivorship (Alpaca's inactive names only; failed listings that left the exchange may be missing - which would flatter the
short side's losses less, not more); few names.

Zero passes = NEWISSUE r1 dead; no other age windows or matching rules are tried.
