information spreads slowly between the two index complexes, so the relative move continues from 10:30 to the close.

RULES
- Data: the house 5-minute RTH masters on raw contract prices (source db_noadj_rth: NQ registry id 37, ES id 33),
  stamped at bar START, clock US/Eastern. The roll-corrected twins (db_adj_rth: NQ 73, ES 63) serve only as a guard: a
  session where roll-corrected minus raw is not constant on either market is skipped (a contract switch inside it).
  Every array is cut to bars before 2025-06-30 BEFORE any signal is computed.
- Eligible session: all 78 bars 09:30-15:55 present on both markets (early closes and gappy sessions skipped).
- Beta: OLS slope (with intercept) of NQ's 5-minute returns on ES's, using the 77 returns inside each session
  (close / previous close - 1 of consecutive bars; no overnight return) of the previous 60 eligible sessions; at least
  40 needed, else no trade.
- Signal at 10:30: s = rN - beta x rE, where rX = close of the 10:25 bar / open of the 09:30 bar - 1 (the first hour).
  Scale = median |s| over the previous 60 eligible sessions (at least 40). Trade if s != 0 and |s| >= k x scale.
- Hedge: h = beta x (NQ price x 20) / (ES price x 50), prices = the 10:25 bar's closes, rounded to 0.1 contract (in
  practice ES plus MES, 1 ES = 10 MES), at least 0.1.
- Trade (CONTINUATION, the side tested): s > 0 -> long 1 NQ and short h ES; s < 0 -> short 1 NQ and long h ES. Entry at
  the open of the 10:30 bar on both markets, exit at the close of the 15:50 bar (15:55) on both. No stop.
  P&L $ = side x [20 x (NQ exit - NQ entry) - 50 x h x (ES exit - ES entry)] - cost.
- Cost per round trip (house rule): NQ 0.533 pt x $20 + h x ES 0.363 pt x $50; stress = +0.25 pt per contract per
  market (NQ +$5, ES +h x $12.50).
- Cells: k in {0, 1, 2} = 3 primary cells. Control, never a pass route: the reversal mirror (same days, opposite side).
- Stretches: EARLY = the first eligible session -> 2016-06-30 (sign check only); WF = 2016-07-01 -> 2025-06-29.
- Family-wide null: each eligible day's side is replaced by a fair coin (one coin per day, shared by the three k cells),
  cost re-applied; statistic = the MAX over the 3 cells of the WF per-trade t; 500 reps, fixed seed.
- Yardstick (owner rule 2026-09-28; house convention of 2026-10-01, BOOK.md 10r): ROC %/yr at a $30,000 worst drawdown
  = 30 x MAR, with net and drawdown of the daily P&L (every eligible session, zeros included) inside the stretch and
  years = (last day - first day) / 365.25; Sortino = mean / RMS of negative daily P&L x sqrt(252), zero days included.
