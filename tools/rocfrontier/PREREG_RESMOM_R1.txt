MECHANISM. Investors under-react to firm-specific news, so stock-specific returns drift for months (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993;
Grundy & Martin 2001). Ranking on RESIDUAL returns - the part a market model does not explain - removes the time-varying market
beta that makes plain momentum crash when the market turns (Daniel & Moskowitz 2016), and roughly doubles momentum's Sharpe
ratio with smaller drawdowns (Blitz, Huij & Martens 2011, J. Empirical Finance; Gutierrez & Pirinsky 2007). Counter-evidence
written in now: momentum has been weak in US large caps in parts of 2016-2025, and residual momentum's published edge is mostly
pre-2010; this test is a fresh, costed, point-in-time read.

- [S1] WHAT THE v2 SCORE IS. W = sessions r-251 .. r (252), F = r-251 .. r-21 (231, the formation), S = r-20 .. r (21, the
  skipped month). The regression over W gives alpha a and beta b; its residuals u_d = r_d - a - b x ES_d sum to zero over W. The
  v2 residual is e_d = r_d - b x ES_d = u_d + a, so the formation sum is EXACTLY sum_F e_d = 231 x a - sum_S u_d = sum_W e_d -
  sum_S e_d: the stock's twelve-month return net of its market beta with the last month taken out (equivalently 231 x its daily
  alpha minus the skipped month's residual). The score divides that sum by the standard deviation of e_d over F (the same as
  u_d's, a being a constant). The harness proves the identity on synthetic data in its selftest.
- [S2] IN-SAMPLE BETA (disclosed): b is fitted over W, which contains the formation months and the skipped month, so the beta
  removed is fitted on the same returns that are ranked - not an out-of-sample beta (Blitz, Huij & Martens use 36 prior months,
  which this cache cannot hold without losing two of the nine WF years). Every input is known at the rank close r, so it is not
  look-ahead; what it changes is that the score is beta-neutral over W by construction. The held positions' realised beta to ES
  is measured after the fill, out of sample, and reported (#463's drawdown weeks first).
