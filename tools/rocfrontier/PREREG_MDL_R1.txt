QUESTION. What must a new leg (or a re-sizing of an existing one) look like before it can lift BOOK #463 past the bars
every lane is judged on - and therefore which hunts cannot matter, whatever they find? Two leg properties decide it to
first order: what the leg earns for its own risk, and what it does while the book is falling. Adding a small amount w of
leg L raises the book's walk-forward ROC @ $30k only if L's yearly net / the book's yearly net > L's loss inside the
book's deepest drawdown / that drawdown's depth (the derivative of 30 x net/DD at w = 0) - so a leg that earns while the
book falls helps at almost any return, and one that falls with the book needs a large return. The map puts numbers on
that, with the noise of real stretches included.

BOOK. #463's daily P&L from r11_risk.py's rule-U records (the house convention); the harness refuses to run unless they
reproduce WF 93.81 / 3.816 and LB 155.54 / 4.150. WF = 2016-07-01 -> 2025-06-29, LB = 2025-06-30 -> 2026-06-30
inclusive. BARS (the ones every lane's book-add stage uses): WF book ROC @ $30k >= 1.05 x 93.81 = 98.5 with Sortino >=
3.816; LB >= 155.54 with Sortino >= 4.150; the leg's size c in {0.5, 1, 2} x its unit, chosen by WF book ROC, frozen.

DRAWDOWN WEEKS (per stretch, from the book's own daily equity, peak starting at 0 at the stretch start): a qualifying
episode = a drawdown at least 1/3 as deep as the stretch's deepest; its days = the day after the peak through the
trough. A DD week = a calendar week with at least 3 of its trading days inside a qualifying episode. Two leg measures:
rho_dd = correlation of weekly sums (leg, book) over the DD weeks; DO (drawdown offset) = the leg's P&L summed over the
qualifying episodes' days / the book's loss over the same days (+ = the leg earns while the book falls).

SYNTHETIC LEGS. On each day the leg trades with probability p = min(1, n / 252); on a trading day its P&L =
mu + sigma_t x (rho_r x z_t + sqrt(1 - rho_r^2) x e_t), where z_t = the book's P&L that day standardised within its regime
(DD days / other days), e_t = an independent standard draw, rho_r = rho_dd on DD days and rho_out on other days; off days
= 0. mu / sigma set the leg's yearly Sharpe s. Unit size: yearly volatility = 1/3 of the book's WF yearly volatility
(CHOICE: the optimal weight of a Sharpe-1 leg next to a Sharpe-3 book). Grid: s in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3};
rho_dd in {-0.6, -0.3, 0, 0.3, 0.6}; rho_out in {0, rho_dd}; n in {25, 100, 250, 1000} trades a year; tails e_t normal
or Student-t(3); volatility sigma_t constant, or proportional to the book's trailing 20-day volatility (a leg on the
same tape, losses clustered with the book's). 1,120 cells x 200 draws, seed 20261004; the WF and LB parts of a draw use
the same parameters. Every draw's realised rho_dd, DO and standalone ROC @ $30k are measured the same way as a real
leg's, and the map is binned on the REALISED values.

OUTPUTS (no pass / fail - a planning map):

  contours in (the leg's standalone ROC @ $30k, rho_dd), and in dollars at #463's scale: 'a new leg must earn >= $X a
  year at a $30k drawdown of its own with rho_dd <= Y to lift #463's walk-forward half the time'. Same for the LB, and
  what no leg on the grid can do.

- The first-order seat rule above, evaluated on #463's actual deepest WF and LB drawdown windows, in dollars.
- RE-SIZING ROW (MANAGER addition 2; the power reference for the tilt rounds): for each of #463's four legs and
  delta in {0.25, 0.5, 1}, the difference series (the leg at 1 + delta minus the leg at 1 = delta x the leg's daily
  P&L, fractional) added to #463: WF and LB ROC @ $30k and Sortino against the bars, and its rho_dd and DO - what

  (DIP ES #452, DIP NQ #433, ORB #239, ORB #257, NQBRD theta 0.70, and others the lanes point to).

SEAT TEST for the forward shadows (DIP #452 / #433, ORB #239 / #257, KEEL, order-flow sizing, S1 / S2 as named on the
PAPER board), read when a shadow reaches 50 closed forward trades, then every further 50:
(a) BACKTEST SEAT: the shadow's daily series added to #463 by the bar rule above on WF and LB; plus rho_dd and DO in
    each stretch, and where it sits on the map at its own trade rate.
(b) FORWARD SEAT: rho_dd and DO against #463's forward paper book, over qualifying forward episodes (at least 1/3 as
    deep as the WF deepest, $14,950); none yet = 'not readable', re-read at the next 50 trades.
(c) READ: SEAT = (a) clears WF and LB and (b) DO >= 0 when readable; NO SEAT = (a) fails WF; otherwise WAIT. An overlay
    (KEEL, order-flow sizing) is judged on its difference series (sized minus raw), as a re-sizing, not a new leg.
    A SEAT is an owner question, never an automatic adoption.

KNOWN LIMITS: the map is conditional on #463's one realised path (its drawdowns are 2020, 2022 and 2025 events, which
is the point - the bars are on that path); synthetic legs have no regime shifts, decay or capacity limits, so the map is
a NECESSARY condition, never a sufficient one; best-of-3 sizing mirrors what a real leg faces at A2; the LB part is one
year, so its P(lift) is mostly noise and is shown beside, never instead of, the WF map.

PRE-DATA ADDENDUM 1 (2026-10-04, before any map cell on #463 was computed; found by the harness's own smoke test on a
synthetic book): standardising z WITHIN each regime removed the book's drawdown-day level, so rho_dd changed only how a
leg co-moves inside drawdowns, not whether it loses while the book falls - its DO stayed flat across rho_dd (0.056 at
-0.6, 0 and +0.6), contradicting this file's own QUESTION. Fix: z_t = (the book's P&L - its mean over the WHOLE
stretch) / the std of its regime, so DD days keep their level below the stretch mean while the daily correlation inside
each regime stays exactly rho_r; and mu is re-centred so the leg's average stays on target: mu = s x V / (252 p) - sigma
x the stretch mean of g_t x rho_r x z_t. Checked before any real number: on the synthetic book a positively correlated
leg now LOSES inside the deepest window, DO falls monotonically as rho_dd rises, and the stretch mean stays within 5% of
target. Everything else unchanged.
