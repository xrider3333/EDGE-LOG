MECHANISM. At a new high of the day, a 5-minute bar that closes DOWN while aggressive buyers still out-traded
aggressive sellers (positive delta) means resting sellers absorbed the buying and still pushed price lower - a
large passive seller (informed traders often trade with limit orders, Kaniel & Liu 2006; CME iceberg orders).
Absorbed breakouts should fail more often than ordinary pullbacks. Mirror at a new low of the day.

COMMON RULES (both parts)
- Clock US/Eastern. Regular hours (RTH) = 09:30-16:00. 5-minute bars start at 09:30, 09:35, ...; a bar's close is
  known at its start + 5 minutes. Decisions at a bar's close, fills at the next bar's open.
- Signal bars: bar starts 10:00 through 15:25 (the opening half hour belongs to ORB's lane).
- SHORT candidate at bar k: high_k above every earlier RTH high of the session (a new high of the day)
  AND close_k < open_k.
  LONG candidate at bar k: low_k below every earlier RTH low of the session AND close_k > open_k.
- Entry: open of the next bar, 1 contract. If that open is already at or through the stop: no trade.
- Stop: short = high_k + 1 tick; long = low_k - 1 tick (tick 0.25 on both). A bar that trades through the stop
  exits at the stop, or at that bar's open if it opened through it.
- Time exit: close of the 6th bar after the signal bar (30 minutes held), or the session's last RTH bar's close
  if that comes first (shortened sessions). Nothing is held past the close.
- LEG view (what would be traded): one position per market per rule; a candidate that appears before the open
  position has exited is ignored (a signal at the exit bar's close is allowed). PER-SIGNAL view: every candidate
  simulated as its own trade - used only for the tag test (F3, F4).
- Costs per round trip: NQ 0.533 pt ($20/pt), ES 0.363 pt ($50/pt) (house: $5.66 commission / multiplier +
  1 tick). Stress: +0.25 pt per round trip.
- Yardstick (owner rule 2026-09-28): ROC %/yr at a $30,000 worst drawdown valued daily = 30 x MAR,
  MAR = (net / years) / worst drawdown of the daily P&L (every trade closes the same day, so daily = closed).
  Sortino = mean / RMS of the negative daily P&L x sqrt(252), zero days included (the frontier lane's so()).

PART F - with order flow (REVERT absorption), the NinjaTrader 10-second capture, FORWARD SHADOW ONLY
- Data: C:\EdgeLog\ohlc\NQ_10s.csv and ES_10s.csv (chart capture, front-month outright, UTC seconds stamped at
  the bar END: stamp T covers [T - 10 s, T)). 5-minute bars are rebuilt from the 10-second bars (open = first,
  high = max, low = min, close = last) and run through the COMMON RULES unchanged.
- Usable session: >= 2,000 RTH 10-second bars, >= 80% of them with buy_vol + sell_vol > 0 (NOISE round 64's
  rule), and no 10-second open more than 0.5% away from the prior close inside RTH (a contract switch). Decided
  from coverage alone; only sessions dated before the day of the read count (a session must be complete).
- Delta share of a 5-minute bar: DS = sum(delta) / sum(buy_vol + sell_vol) over its 10-second bars; undefined if
  that sum is 0 or fewer than 80% of its volume-bearing 10-second bars carry buy + sell > 0. Only the SIGN of DS is
  used (Tick-Replay backfill gets the direction right and magnitudes only roughly).
- Variant (absorption) = a candidate whose DS opposes the push: SHORT candidates with DS > 0, LONG candidates with
  DS < 0. Twin = the same rule on the same capture bars without the DS condition (every candidate).
- Cells F-NQ and F-ES, judged POOLED (one leg per market, 1 contract each, P&L summed by day over the usable
  sessions) plus a per-market direction check.
- HISTORICAL READ (usable sessions 2026-06-24 -> 2026-09-29, about 45 per market): DESCRIPTIVE ONLY. It prints the
  forward bar's quantities for the record and decides nothing; the shadow runs regardless. (Signal counts seen:
  about 0.8 absorption candidates per session on NQ, 1.0 on ES, before the one-position rule.)
- FORWARD SHADOW - the real test. From 2026-10-01 the capture keeps accumulating and the harness scores it after
  the fact; nothing live changes. Until the read, the harness prints trade COUNTS only. Read ONCE, when the pooled
  variant legs have >= 150 trades on usable forward sessions (estimate 5-7 months; sooner if Paper: NT8 fixes the
  live delta dropouts), not before. PASS only if ALL hold on the forward sessions alone:
    F1. the variant legs beat the twin legs on ROC @ $30k AND on Sortino (same sessions, pooled);
    F2. variant net > 0 after cost, > 0 at +0.25 pt, and > 0 without its single biggest trade;
    F3. per-signal view: the variant candidates' mean $ is at or above the 95th percentile of 2,000 within-session
        random same-size draws from the twin's candidates that have a defined DS (the tag must beat its shape);
    F4. per market: the variant's per-signal mean $ is above the twin's, on NQ and on ES separately;
    F5. binding if BOOK #463's forward daily P&L exists by then (paper nightly book or a re-run): #463 plus the
        variant at 1 contract per market has a higher ROC @ $30k AND Sortino than #463 alone on the same days;
        otherwise it is reported as not available.
  A pass earns a no-order paper shadow leg on the live capture plus an owner call - never an adoption from this
