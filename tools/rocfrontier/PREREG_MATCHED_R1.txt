MATCHED READ r1 - can a forward read decide a shadow line whose gain is a smaller drawdown, if the statistic is matched to the
mechanism? Written 2026-10-05 (FRONTIER cloud lane) before any number of this round; a planning computation on the WALK-FORWARD
ONLY (no lockbox row, no forward P&L). Posted for MANAGER review; nothing runs until GO. The harness tools/rocfrontier/r17_matched.py
binds to this file's canonical-LF sha256 (PREREG_SHA, written in before `parity` runs) and refuses to run while it reads TBD. A change
after the first draw is a dated pre-data addendum or a new file (r2).

WHY. Q16 (BOOK.md 10y) showed that the registered forward rules - ROC at $30k with a margin, and summed dollars - cannot decide
ORB314, ORB239, Q4 or VT within 36 months: their walk-forward gain over #463 is a smaller drawdown with about the same money, and a
mean-type statistic has 1-7% power on a difference whose mean is near zero. The owner therefore ruled (10aa) that the forward lines
are harm monitors. But a smaller giveback is not invisible: it is a claim about the LEFT TAIL of the changed leg's daily results on
the same days, and a paired scale statistic on that tail has power where a paired mean has none. The paired-stop pre-registration
already made the case for pairing ("judging the per-trade difference removes the market noise both arms share"); it chose a mean
because the lines it covered claim more money. This round asks the matched question for the lines that claim less giveback, and
for KEEL (which claims more money in few trades) so that the family is read together.

QUESTION. For ORB314 and ORB239 (the ORB seat; every other leg identical to #463's) and KEEL (the TTM seat): what is the power of a
forward read at 12 / 24 / 36 months on a statistic matched to each line's claim, with the family-wise false pass over the three
lines held at 5%, and which read length, if any, first gives power 0.5 and 0.8? VT (dead on mechanism, RISK r1) and Q4 (post-hoc)
are not read: harm monitors only, as ruled.

DATA (walk-forward 2016-07-01..2025-06-29, weekday rows as Q16 built them). A manifest CSV exported on the box from Q16's parity
rebuild and the leg caches: date; b463 (the book, valued daily); orb463 (the ORB leg of #463 as sized in the book); orb314 and
orb239 (the ORB #314 and #239 legs as sized in their lines); keel_d (KEEL's daily paired difference against #463: the sum over that
day's TTM trades of d_i per the paired-stop pre-registration, zero on days without a TTM trade, blank before 2016-07-27);
keel_line (optional, for KEEL's parity on its own convention). No forward row, no lockbox row; the harness refuses either.

PARITY (before any draw; READY written only if it passes). b463 must reproduce 93.81 / 3.816 / $44,849 (ROC at $30k, Sortino,
worst drawdown; unified convention; within 0.06 / 0.01 / $1); b463 - orb463 + orb314 must reproduce ORB314's recorded 125.6 / 3.95 /
$33,735 and b463 - orb463 + orb239 must reproduce ORB239's 101.8 / 3.84 / $41,319 (BOOK.md 10r, Q16); keel_line, if supplied,
KEEL's 118.545 / 4.2755 / $41,376 on keel_eval's own convention (a looser tolerance, labelled as such). A line that misses parity is
left out and named; if the book misses, nothing runs.

STATISTICS (a = the ORB leg of #463, b = the line's ORB leg, over a read window of n rows).
  M1 DOWNSIDE RATIO - primary for ORB314 and ORB239: DSD(b) / DSD(a) - 1, DSD(x) = sqrt(mean(min(x, 0)^2)), Sortino's own
     denominator; the claim is that it is below zero.
  M2 LOSS-DAY GAIN: mean(b - a) on the days the reference leg lost; the claim is above zero.
  M3 TAIL: the 5% quantile of b minus that of a; the claim is above zero.
  R2 DOLLARS: Q16's summed-dollars rule, for contrast only.
  KEEL - primary T: the registered paired form mean(keel_d) / (sd / sqrt(n)); the claim is above zero (its matched statistic IS
     the mean).
NULL (no edge, fixed now): circular block bootstrap of the JOINT walk-forward rows (every column together), block 21, 20,000 draws
of 756 rows, numpy default_rng(20261020), chunks of 2,500; inside a draw each block takes ONE swap flag with probability one half,
shared by every line: in a swapped block the pair (a, b) is exchanged for both ORB lines and keel_d changes sign. That is the
within-pair exchangeability null: the line and the reference leg have the same distribution day by day, and only the days on which
they differ carry information. TRUTH: the same draws without swaps (the walk-forward edge taken at face value, an UPPER bound - the
lines were chosen on the walk-forward), and at HALF the edge (b replaced by a + 0.5 x (b - a); keel_d halved).
FAMILY: per statistic kind and horizon, each line's statistic is standardised by its own null spread (sign set so the claim's
direction is positive) and the family-wise 5% critical value is the 95th percentile, over null draws, of the maximum over the three
lines. Single false pass per line and power at full and half edge are read against that one critical value.

PRINTED. Parity; per line x statistic x horizon: single false pass, critical value, power at full and half edge; the HEADLINE table
last: per line, the read length (12 / 24 / 36 months, else "> 36 months") at which the primary statistic first reaches power 0.5
and 0.8, at full and half edge, beside Q16's months (> 36 for all three); and, report only, the minimum track record length
(Bailey and Lopez de Prado 2012) of each line's paired difference, and M1 with and without each leg's single worst day.

WHAT IT DECIDES. Nothing is adopted, killed or read forward. If a line's matched read reaches power 0.8 at full edge and 0.5 at
half edge within 36 months with the family-wise false pass at 5%, this round PROPOSES to the owner via MANAGER that the matched
statistic become that line's registered deciding forward read at that length (written down before the read date; the paired
stops stay as harm monitors); the owner decides. If no line gets there, the lines stay harm monitors and this file records why.
Forward P&L is not read by this round in any case.

COULD FOOL US. The statistic was chosen after the walk-forward gains were seen; power is an upper bound and is printed at half
edge for that reason. The null swaps whole three-week blocks, which keeps dependence but assumes the two legs are exchangeable
inside a block under no edge - a leg that trades on different days than the reference breaks pairing, which is why only same-seat
lines (same signals, different management) are read here. A forward read on a scale statistic can be moved by one extreme day; M1
is printed with and without the single worst day of each leg (report only). The 5% family-wise rate is per read, not per look at
the forward record: if the paired stops look every ten trades, the matched read is still taken ONCE at its registered length.
