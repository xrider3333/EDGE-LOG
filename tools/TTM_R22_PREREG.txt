THE RULE (honest bar timing, no intrabar peeking): R = the trade's initial risk in points = |entry price -
initial structural stop|. After a bar CLOSES with the open trade at least +1R in its favour (close vs entry), the
stop moves to the ENTRY price from the NEXT bar on (never loosened; the entry bar's own close counts). A later bar
that trades through it exits at the stop, or at the open when the open gaps past it (the file's existing rule).
Two variants, both pre-registered:
  22A = breakeven, then the strategy's NORMAL exits (fade after 2 bars, the session close) - the owner's words read
        literally, since TTM has no fixed target;
  22B = breakeven, then RIDE: once the stop is at breakeven, the fade exit is switched off and the trade runs to the
        breakeven stop or the session close.
Implemented by swapping the structural-stop simulator inside the #459 module chain for a copy with the rule added;
with the rule switched off the copy must reproduce the twin to the trade.

YARDSTICK (owner, 09-28): ROC %/yr at a $30k worst drawdown valued daily (= 30 x MAR), WF and LB apart, Sortino,
on DOLLARS - the known trap is that a breakeven rule flatters R-per-trade stats while costing dollars (memory: EV R
is gameable by the breakeven), so R statistics are reported for information only and decide nothing.
PASS (triage), per variant, all of: (a) ROC@$30k >= the twin in WF AND LB; (b) Sortino >= the twin in WF AND LB;
(c) >= 100 WF trades; (d) LB net > 0 without its single biggest trade. The 50-LB-trade minimum cannot be met by this
leg (the twin has 15 LB trades); per the owner's 09-29 rule TTM candidates are judged on walk-forward plus the
validate and a book-level test. Two variants are tried, so a pass must hold for the variant on its own merits and
is reported beside the other.
A pass goes to the house Auto-Validate (new strategy file, ADJ master pinned, 2010-06-07 .. 2026-06-30, 900
