MULTIPLE COMPARISONS, stated before anyone reads a p-value: TEN rules were tested here, so the
smallest honest threshold is 0.05 divided by 10, which is 0.005. NO rule in this table clears that.
A p-value near 0.03 with seven attempts is what chance produces; the holdout and lockbox columns are
the evidence that matters, and they matter only where the bucket is big enough to mean anything.

HOW TO READ THIS: a rule earns attention only if it beats the base row in BOTH holdout and lockbox,
and the tilt rows are the shape this shop adopts - a selection rule that throws trades away has lost
money every time it has been tried in this family. Bucket sizes matter: under about 40 trades a
difference is not measurable on this sample at all.
