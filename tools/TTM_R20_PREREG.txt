IDEA 20a - ORDER FLOW BEHIND THE FIRE (quality)
Mechanism: a squeeze release that aggressive traders are pushing (buyers lifting offers on a long fire,
sellers hitting bids on a short fire) is a real breakout; a release against the tape is more likely a fake
that fades - which is exactly the trade the one-bar fade exit already cuts.
Measure (known at the FIRE bar's close = the signal bar, nothing from the fill bar): imbalance =
sum(delta) / sum(buy_vol + sell_vol) over the 10-second rows inside the fire bar. NinjaTrader stamps a
10s row at its END, so a row stamped e belongs to the bar starting s when s < e <= s + bar length.
Coverage = rows present with order flow / rows expected; a fire with coverage < 0.8, or whose 10s closes
disagree with the master's bar close by more than 2 ticks (wrong contract around a roll), is "no data".
Agree = sign(imbalance) equals the trade side; imbalance exactly 0 counts as disagree.
Universe: the frozen #299 crown (TTMSQZ_3_0.py, the clean ATR engine, settings as in round 19, NOT re-tuned)
on six cells: ES and NQ x 5m / 15m / 30m RTH, NO-ADJUST masters (db_noadj_rth - the capture is the
unadjusted front month), costs ES 0.363 pts $50, NQ 0.533 pts $20, one contract. Per-trade outcome for
pooling across cells = R = net points / (1.5 x ATR at the fire bar) (the trade's own stop distance).
In-hand read (2026-06-23 .. last full session): DESCRIPTIVE ONLY - agree vs disagree count, mean R,
net $, and the agree-only 6-cell book vs the all-fires book (daily P&L, same yardstick). No verdict
on this stretch can adopt anything. It decides one thing: the forward shadow starts only if agree mean R
> disagree mean R here (a weak screen so a dead idea does not occupy a shadow slot).
