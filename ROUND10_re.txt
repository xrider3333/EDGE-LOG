1. ALL 8 re-entry configs FAIL. Every one clears G2 (net >= 95% of $397,150 — re-entries do add gross
   dollars, +$1.5k to +$5.5k on the full window) but every one FAILS G1 (5-year MAR drops from 2.79 to
   2.12-2.65, never higher) and every non-CROWN row also FAILS G3 (OOS PF drops from 1.531 to 1.424-1.514).
   Re-entry adds trades and a little net $, but adds proportionally MORE drawdown and MORE variance —
   it is a worse risk-adjusted trade, not a free option.

2. THE RE-ENTRY TRADES THEMSELVES HAVE ~NO EDGE: isolated on the best-MAR config (re=2/stop/rebreak),
   the 205 re-entry trades net only $5,487 on PF 1.073 and 39.0% win rate, vs the 2299 first trades'
   $397,150 net / PF 1.371 / 45.1% win rate. The second same-direction breakout after a stop-out is a
   materially weaker signal than the first — it costs COST=0.533 pts/RT per shot and barely covers it.

3. "stop" vs "any" made NO DIFFERENCE at any reentry_max in this crown config — re=1/stop/rebreak and
   re=1/any/rebreak are IDENTICAL rows (2494 trades, same $ down to the dollar) in every window, same
   for re=2. Reason: target_R=5.0 with stop_frac=2.5 means a target hit is a 2R+ winner; in the data,
   sessions that hit that target essentially never had bars left afterward to also throw a fresh
   same-direction close beyond up_lvl/dn_lvl before EOD, so the "any" gate's extra permission (allow
   re-entry after a TARGET exit too) was never actually exercised.

4. "rebreak" clearly beats "retest" at every reentry_max: re=1/stop/rebreak (5y MAR 2.65, OOS PF 1.514)
   vs re=1/stop/retest (5y MAR 2.19, OOS PF 1.448); re=2/stop/rebreak (2.65 / 1.487) vs re=2/stop/retest
   (2.12 / 1.424). Requiring price to close back inside the range before allowing a fresh breakout
   filters out the "still chopping around the level" whipsaw that "retest" happily re-enters on — but
   even the better mode still loses to not re-entering at all.

5. PLATEAU is moot/NOT-PLATEAU: the single best-by-5y-MAR row (re=2/stop/rebreak, MAR 2.65) is already
   BELOW the crown's own MAR (2.79), so there is no positive edge to plateau-check — the whole re-entry
   knob-space is dominated by reentry_max=0. VERDICT: same-direction re-entry on the #314 crown is
   CLOSED — do not adopt, do not re-sweep this shape without a materially different re-entry signal
   (e.g. a fresh independent range/level rather than the same up_lvl/dn_lvl).
