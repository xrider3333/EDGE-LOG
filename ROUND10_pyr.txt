5-BULLET READ
====================================================================================================================================
1. Pyramiding raises net across nearly every config (crown $397,150 -> up to $582,578 at
   addR=0.5/shared/max2) but that is mostly LEVERAGE: avg units/trade climbs to 1.03-1.60,
   and once net/DD are divided back down to a per-unit basis, only a narrow band (addR=0.5
   at max1, and addR=2.0 at any stop/max) shows a REAL MAR improvement (0.85 -> 0.88-0.92);
   everything at addR=1.0-1.5 or at max2 sizing is "net only (leverage)" or worse.
2. 2 of 20 configs clear gates G1/G2/G3/G5 outright: addR=2.0/shared/max1 (full MAR 0.92,
   5y MAR 3.04) and addR=2.0/own/max1 (full MAR 0.90, 5y MAR 3.00) - both are single-add,
   max1 configs; every max2 (second-add) variant FAILS, mostly on G3 (OOS PF/net below the
   crown) because the second add fires rarely (n_adds in the tens to low hundreds) and its
   own edge is thin-to-negative at the wider addR thresholds.
3. G4 (plateau) KILLS both gate-passing survivors: addR=2.0/shared/max1's neighbour on
   pyr_max (max2) keeps -214% of its 5y-MAR gain over the crown (net NEGATIVE gain), and its
   addR=3.0 neighbour keeps -96% - the "add trigger" and "how many adds" knobs are both
   cliffs, not a plateau. Result: 0/20 configs pass all 5 gates.
4. The added units' own book (FULL window, ADDED UNITS ONLY table) shows the edge decays
   with distance: addR=0.5 adds are consistently profitable (PF 1.2-1.4, biggest single
   contributor to net, 978-1375 fills) but addR=3.0 adds are a small LOSING sideshow (21
   fills, -$6.2k, PF 0.65) - the "add into strength" idea only pays close to entry, which
   is also where G1's full-MAR gate is hardest to clear (the extra units drag DD up before
   the win-rate advantage of an early add offsets it).
5. VERDICT: pyramiding is NOT adopted from this round. It reliably manufactures more net
   dollars (that is real, tradeable money) but the fairness-scaled read shows most of it is
   leverage rather than a sharper edge, the two spots where MAR genuinely improves both fail
   the pre-registered plateau gate, and the crown ($397,150 full / MAR 0.85, MAR 2.79 5y)
   remains the reference. If this family is revisited, addR in the 0.4-0.6 zone at max1
   (where the added units themselves are profitable, PF>1.2, and MAR does improve on a
   per-unit basis) is the only region worth re-fencing with a tighter grid.
